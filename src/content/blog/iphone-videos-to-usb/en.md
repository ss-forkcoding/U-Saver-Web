## Key takeaways

You can transfer video from an iPhone to a USB drive without a computer. Select the video in the Photos app and choose the drive as the save location from the Share menu.

Videos over 4GB won't fit on a FAT32 drive. To move long videos, the drive needs to be formatted as exFAT.

"Export Unmodified Originals" saves the video as it was before any edits. If you need the trimmed or edited version, use "Save to Files."

Before deleting a video from your iPhone, play the copy on the drive through to the end.

"Only my 30-minute video won't save." "I trimmed a clip, but the copy on the drive plays from the start." These problems show up with video far more often than with photos.

A single video can be a very large file, and saving one takes time. Check the file size and the drive format before you begin.

## What should you check before you transfer video from iPhone to USB?

Check the video's size, the drive's format, and the free space on the drive. To see a video's size, open it in Photos and tap the Info button.

| Video size | exFAT drive | FAT32 drive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4GB or less | Can be stored | Can be stored |
| Over 4GB | Can be stored | Can't be stored |

[Apple notes](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/record-videos-iph61f49e4bb/ios) that higher resolutions and faster frame rates produce larger files. If you shot a long video in 4K, check its size first.

[Apple's guide to external storage](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios) lists exFAT and FAT32 among the supported formats. NTFS isn't on the list.

If iCloud Photos is set to Optimize iPhone Storage, the original video may be in iCloud. Downloading it adds time, so connect to Wi-Fi before you start.

## How to transfer video from iPhone to USB in 3 steps

### Step 1. Pick your videos from the Videos album

Plug in the drive and open Photos. Under Collections, go to Media Types and tap Videos to see only your videos.

Start with just a few. If a large batch of long videos stops partway, it's hard to tell how far it got.

### Step 2. Choose how to save from the Share menu

Tap the Share button and choose one of the two options below. For a video you never edited, export the original.

| Option | What ends up on the drive | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Export Unmodified Originals | The video as captured | Keeping videos you didn't edit |
| Save to Files | The video with your edits | Keeping a trimmed or edited version |

### Step 3. Select the drive and wait for saving to finish

Pick your drive under Locations and tap Save. If the window opens to iCloud Drive, tap the Back arrow to reach Locations.

Leave the drive plugged in until saving finishes. Video takes longer than photos because the files are bigger.

## How do you check the video on the USB drive?

Open the drive in the Files app and play your longest video. Skip to the last section and confirm it plays to the end.

- The file size on the drive is close to the size you saw in Photos.
- The final part of a long video plays.
- Every video you selected is on the drive.
- Important videos also exist somewhere other than this drive.

If iCloud Photos is on, deleting a video also removes it from other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. If you're trying to free up space, check that setting before you delete anything.

## What if the video won't play on a Windows PC?

Depending on your camera settings, iPhone videos are saved in HEVC (H.265). On a Windows PC without an HEVC codec, the video may play sound only or refuse to play.

Install an HEVC codec on the PC or use a player that supports HEVC. For future videos, you can choose Most Compatible under Settings, Camera, Formats. Those files are larger.

## FAQ: transferring video from iPhone to USB

### Does transferring video from iPhone to USB reduce quality?

No, not with "Export Unmodified Originals." That option saves the file as it was captured.

### Why won't my iPhone video over 4GB save to USB?

A FAT32 drive can't store files over 4GB. Use an exFAT drive. Reformatting erases the drive, so copy its contents somewhere else first.

### I trimmed a video, but the full-length version was saved. Why?

"Export Unmodified Originals" saves the video from before your edits. Save it again with "Save to Files" from the Share menu.

### Can you transfer video from iPhone to USB without a computer?

Yes. Connect the drive to your iPhone and use only the Photos and Files apps. To compare options for other data, see [how to back up an iPhone without a computer](/blog/en/backup-iphone-without-computer).
