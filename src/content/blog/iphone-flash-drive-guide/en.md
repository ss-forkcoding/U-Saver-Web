## Key takeaways

To use a flash drive with an iPhone, plug it into the charging port and open the Files app. On the Browse screen, tap the drive's name under Locations to see what's on it.

The drive needs a single data partition. If you format it as exFAT or FAT32, both the iPhone and a PC can read it.

Photos and videos don't appear in the Files app. They need to be exported to the drive from the Photos app.

Remove the drive only after copying has finished.

"I plugged it in and nothing happened." "Where do I see the files on the drive?" These are usually the first questions after connecting a flash drive to an iPhone.

An iPhone shows no alert or window when you connect a drive. You have to open the Files app yourself to see it.

## Which flash drive works with an iPhone?

A flash drive works with an iPhone if the connector, partition, and format all fit. Check these three things.

| What to check | Requirement | If it doesn't match |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connector | USB-C drive for a USB-C iPhone, camera adapter for a Lightning iPhone | Get an adapter |
| Partition | One data partition | Reformat as a single partition |
| Format | exFAT, FAT32, APFS, or macOS Extended (HFS+) | Reformat to a supported format |

These requirements come from [Apple's guide to external storage](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios). NTFS isn't on Apple's list.

Some drives sold specifically for iPhone need the manufacturer's app before you can open files. Check the product description before you buy. If you also use a Windows PC, exFAT is convenient. FAT32 can't store files over 4GB.

## How to use a flash drive with an iPhone in 4 steps

### Step 1. Plug the drive into the charging port

A USB-C iPhone takes a USB-C drive directly. For a Lightning iPhone, plug the drive into a camera adapter and connect the adapter to the iPhone. To choose an adapter, see [iPhone OTG USB connection requirements](/blog/en/iphone-otg-usb).

### Step 2. Open the drive from Locations in the Files app

Open the Files app and tap Browse. Tap the drive's name under Locations to see its folders and files. If you don't see Locations, tap Browse again.

### Step 3. Copy or move your files

Touch and hold a file, then choose Copy. Open the drive, touch and hold an empty area, and choose Paste.

For several files, tap the More button at the top right, tap Select, and pick the files. Use the Move button at the bottom to choose where they go.

### Step 4. Remove the drive after copying finishes

Apple's guide only describes removing the drive from the charging port, and it doesn't mention an eject button. Pulling the drive mid-copy can leave files missing or damaged, so wait for the progress indicator to disappear.

## Photos and videos move through the Photos app

iPhone photos live in the Photos app. Select them there and save them to the drive from the Share menu.

The steps are in [how to transfer photos from iPhone to USB](/blog/en/iphone-photos-to-usb). For long videos, see [how to transfer video from iPhone to USB](/blog/en/iphone-videos-to-usb).

## What should you check if the flash drive doesn't show up?

If the drive's name isn't under Locations in the Files app, go through this list in order.

| Order | What to check | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether it's fully seated | Reconnect the adapter and drive |
| 2 | The drive's format | Check it on a PC or Mac, and reformat if it's NTFS |
| 3 | The number of partitions | Reformat multiple partitions into one |
| 4 | Power | Connect a powered hub or adapter |

Reformatting erases everything on the drive. If it holds files, copy them somewhere else first.

## FAQ: how to use a flash drive for iPhone

### Why does nothing happen when I plug in a flash drive?

That's normal. An iPhone doesn't open a window when you connect a drive. Look for the drive's name on the Browse screen in the Files app.

### Can I format a flash drive on an iPhone?

Yes. [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios) says you can change the format using the Files app or a Mac or PC. Formatting erases everything on the drive.

### Can I charge my iPhone with a flash drive plugged in?

The iPhone has one port, so you need extra hardware. Use a powered hub with a USB-C iPhone, or a Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter with a USB power adapter on a Lightning iPhone.

### Are files on a flash drive safe?

If you lose the drive, someone else can open the files on it. Keep a second copy of anything important, and store the drive somewhere secure.
