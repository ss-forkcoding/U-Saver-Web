## Key takeaways

Not every Galaxy phone takes an SD card. In Samsung's official table, the Galaxy S series takes microSD up to the S20 line, and the S21 line has no external memory. Samsung Japan also lists the Z Flip and Z Fold series as unsupported.

If your model takes a card, you format, unmount, and encrypt it from the Storage screen in Settings. Menu paths differ by One UI version and model.

Unmount the card before you take it out. Samsung Japan says removing it without unmounting can damage the saved data.

"I bought a new Galaxy and I can't find the SD card slot." "I put a card in, but where do I manage it in Settings?" These are common questions from people handling a Galaxy SD card for the first time. This guide covers how to check whether your Galaxy has a slot and how to manage the card from the Settings menu. Moving photos to the card is covered in [moving Android photos to an SD card](/blog/en/android-photos-to-sd), and general card use on Android phones in [micro SD card for phone](/blog/en/android-microsd).

## Does my Galaxy have an SD card slot?

It depends on the model. [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) says not all Samsung devices support microSD cards, and models without support only have a slot for the SIM card.

Here is part of the S series table from [Samsung UK support](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/what-size-memory-card-does-my-smartphone-accept/).

| Model | Max external memory |
| --- | --- |
| Galaxy S6, S6 edge, S6 edge+ | No external memory |
| Galaxy S7, S7 edge | Up to 200GB |
| Galaxy S8, S8+ | Up to 256GB |
| Galaxy S9, S9+ | Up to 400GB |
| Galaxy S10, S10+, S10e | Up to 512GB |
| Galaxy S20, S20+, S20 Ultra | Up to 1TB |
| Galaxy S21 5G, S21+ 5G, S21 Ultra 5G | No external memory |

This table covers UK and Ireland models. The same page says specifications can differ by region. [Samsung Japan support](https://www.samsung.com/jp/support/mobile-devices/tell-me-a-micro-sd-card-or-sim-card-that-can-be-used-in-the-device/) lists the S21 series and later, the Z Flip series, the Z Fold series, the A36 5G, and the A57 5G as not supporting external SD cards for Japanese models. In the same table, the A25 5G takes up to 1.5TB and the A55 5G up to 1TB.

To check your own model:

1. Find the model name in Settings > About phone.
2. Look for the external memory entry on that model's spec page on the Samsung site.
3. Note the maximum capacity. Pick a card within it.

Capacity standards are explained in [SDHC card vs SDXC vs SDUC](/blog/en/sdhc-vs-sdxc). This guide doesn't test or recommend any specific card.

## What does a Galaxy SD card tray look like?

On most models the card shares the SIM tray. [Samsung UK support](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/using-an-sd-card/) describes these tray types.

| Tray type | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Hybrid tray | Two SIMs, or one SIM and one SD card |
| Dual SIM tray | Two SIMs |
| Single SIM tray | One SIM |

The tray has markings that show where the SD card goes. Place the card with the gold contacts facing down. [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) says to check the tray outline so you don't push the ejector tool into a microphone or speaker hole. If the tray won't come out or gets damaged, Samsung says service may be necessary.

Models with a removable battery work differently. According to the same US page, on the XCover6 Pro you turn the phone off, remove the back cover and battery, and insert the card in its slot right on top of the SIM slot.

## Where is the SD card in Galaxy Settings?

Go to Settings > Battery and device care > Storage. [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) says to swipe left on Internal storage at the top of the screen until you see the SD card screen. [Samsung Japan support](https://www.samsung.com/jp/support/mobile-devices/how-do-i-dismount-an-external-sd-card-of-galaxy/) describes tapping the internal storage field in Storage and then choosing external storage.

Here is what you can do from there.

| Task | Where in Settings | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Format the card | Storage > SD card > More options > Format | [Samsung US](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) |
| Unmount | Storage > SD card (external storage) > More options > Unmount | [Samsung Japan](https://www.samsung.com/jp/support/mobile-devices/how-do-i-dismount-an-external-sd-card-of-galaxy/) |
| Encrypt or decrypt | Search Settings for Encrypt SD card or Decrypt SD card | [Samsung US](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) |
| Change an app's storage | Settings > Apps > choose the app > Storage > Change | [Samsung US](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) |

Depending on the One UI version, the menu may read Device care or Battery and device care. The same US page says Samsung Android devices don't support Adoptable storage, which merges the card into internal storage. The card shows up as separate storage.

## How do I format an SD card on a Galaxy?

Format it in Settings. Formatting erases all data on the card, so copy what you need to a computer first. This is the path from [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/):

1. Open Settings, then tap Battery and device care.
2. Tap Storage.
3. Swipe left on Internal storage until you see the SD card screen.
4. Tap More options (three vertical dots), then tap Format.
5. Tap Format again when prompted.

[Samsung Korea's service center guide](https://www.samsungsvc.co.kr/solution/618136) gives a different path for some models: Storage > More > Advanced > SD card > Format. You'll see one or the other depending on your model.

Card formats follow these rules.

| Item | What Samsung says | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compatible formats | FAT32 or exFAT, exFAT preferred | [Samsung US](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) |
| FAT32 | Only on cards under 32GB; no files over 4GB | Same page |
| NTFS and APFS | Not compatible | Same page |
| Default by size | FAT32 up to 32GB, exFAT from 64GB | [Samsung Korea service center](https://www.samsungsvc.co.kr/solution/39935) |

Formatting on a computer is covered in [how to format a micro SD card](/blog/en/format-microsd).

## How do I unmount a Galaxy SD card?

Unmount it in Settings before you take it out. These steps from [Samsung Japan support](https://www.samsung.com/jp/support/mobile-devices/how-do-i-dismount-an-external-sd-card-of-galaxy/) are based on a Galaxy A55 (Android 16, One UI 8.5).

1. Open the Settings app.
2. Tap Device care.
3. Tap Storage.
4. Tap the internal storage field.
5. Tap external storage.
6. Tap the three dots (More options) at the top right.
7. Tap Unmount. Once it's unmounted, the SD card details disappear.

If that path doesn't work, the same page says to open the SD card in the My Files app and tap the three dots > Unmount. Use My Files as well when you've connected an external card reader. [Samsung Korea's service center](https://www.samsungsvc.co.kr/solution/39935) says removing a card improperly can damage it.

## What changes when I encrypt a Galaxy SD card?

Only the phone that encrypted the card can read it. These steps are from [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/).

1. Connect the phone to its charger. Encrypting and decrypting drain the battery quickly.
2. Open Settings, then search for and select Encrypt SD card or Decrypt SD card.
3. Tap Encrypt SD card or Decrypt SD card.
4. If you have a lock screen set up, enter your credentials.
5. Wait for it to finish. It can take several minutes, depending on the card size and the amount of data.

The same page says that if the phone is factory reset while the card is encrypted, that phone can no longer read the card. Decrypt it before a reset or before using the card in a computer or another phone.

## What if my Galaxy doesn't detect the SD card?

Check in this order, based on [Samsung Korea's service center guide](https://www.samsungsvc.co.kr/solution/618136).

| Step | What to do | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Update the software | Settings > Software update > Download and install |
| 2 | Reinsert the card | Turn the phone off, remove the card, check for damage or debris, wipe it with a soft cloth, and put it back |
| 3 | Try another device | See whether another device detects the card |
| 4 | Force restart | Use the side and volume down buttons to turn the phone off and on |
| 5 | Safe mode | If the card works in safe mode, try removing recently installed apps |
| 6 | Format | Copy your data to a computer, then format the card in Settings |
| 7 | Phone diagnostics | Check the SD card item in Phone diagnostics in the Samsung Members app |

If other phones and PCs can't read the card either, [Samsung Korea's service center](https://www.samsungsvc.co.kr/solution/39935) says the card may be damaged and suggests an inspection at a service center. The same page notes that service centers don't move data for you, so back it up yourself. More checks are in [SD card corrupted?](/blog/en/sd-card-not-working)

## How can I use an SD card on a Galaxy without a slot?

You can connect a USB-C card reader. [Samsung Japan support](https://www.samsung.com/jp/support/mobile-devices/how-do-i-dismount-an-external-sd-card-of-galaxy/) says to unmount from the My Files app when you use an external card reader. Readers behave differently, so check the reader maker's instructions. Opening a card on a computer is covered in [how to connect an SD card to a PC](/blog/en/sd-card-to-pc).

## Does the iPhone have an SD card slot?

No. On an iPhone, you connect a card reader to the charging port and open the card in the Files app. A card you used as exFAT on a Galaxy fits the formats listed in [Apple's external storage guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios): APFS, macOS Extended (HFS+), exFAT, FAT32, and FAT. How to connect one is covered in [can you plug an SD card into an iPhone?](/blog/en/iphone-sd-card-slot) and [micro SD for iPhone](/blog/en/iphone-microsd).

## FAQ: Galaxy SD card

### Can I put an SD card in a Galaxy S24 or S25?

In Samsung UK's table, the S21 line has no external memory. Samsung Japan also lists the S21 series and later as unsupported. Check the exact spec on Samsung's page for your model.

### Can a Galaxy merge an SD card into internal storage?

Samsung US support says Samsung Android devices don't support this feature (Adoptable storage). The card works as separate storage.

### Do I lose a SIM slot when I add an SD card?

On a hybrid tray, yes. According to Samsung UK, a hybrid tray holds two SIMs, or one SIM and one SD card.

### Can I use a card I formatted on my computer?

FAT32 and exFAT are compatible formats. Samsung US says NTFS and APFS are not. If the phone has trouble reading it, move your data off the card and format it again in Galaxy Settings.

### What happens if I remove the card without unmounting it?

Samsung Japan says the saved data can be damaged. Unmount the card in Settings or My Files before you take it out.
