## Key takeaways

A micro SD card for phone storage goes into the phone's card tray. Not every phone has a slot. First check whether your model supports microSD and how large a card it accepts.

When you insert a card, Android asks you to format it as portable storage or internal storage. If you plan to move the card to a computer or another device, choose portable storage.

Before you pull the card out, unmount it in Settings. An iPhone has no card slot, so iPhone users should read [micro SD for iPhone](/blog/en/iphone-microsd).

"Can I buy any micro SD card for my phone?" "I put a card in, but my photos still save to the phone." These are common questions from people adding a microSD card to a phone for the first time. This guide covers inserting the card, formatting it, and keeping photos and files on it on an Android phone. Using microSD in other devices is covered in [how do I use a micro SD card?](/blog/en/how-to-use-microsd)

## Can my phone use a micro SD card?

It depends on the model. [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) says not all phones and tablets support microSD cards, and models without support only have a slot for the SIM card. [Samsung UK support](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/using-an-sd-card/) also says the size and maximum capacity of the card you can use differ between devices and models.

Check in this order:

1. Find the memory card entry in your phone's manual or the maker's spec page.
2. If it's supported, note the maximum capacity.
3. Pull out the tray and look for a microSD space next to the SIM space. According to Samsung UK, some trays are hybrid: they take two SIM cards, or one SIM and one SD card. On those, a card takes the place of the second SIM.

## Which micro SD card for phone use should I pick?

Pick one within your phone's maximum capacity. Here are the card types from [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/).

| Card type | Capacity | Use in Samsung's guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| microSD | Up to 2GB | Light use such as still photos |
| microSDHC | Up to 32GB | Light use such as still photos |
| microSDXC | Over 32GB, up to 2TB | Recommended for high-resolution video |

The card standard goes up to 2TB, but your phone has its own maximum. Capacity standards are explained in [SDHC card vs SDXC vs SDUC](/blog/en/sdhc-vs-sdxc), and speed markings in [how to read SD card speed classes](/blog/en/sd-card-speed-class). If you plan to keep apps on the card, also see [A2 SD card vs A1](/blog/en/sd-card-a1-a2). This guide doesn't test or recommend any specific card.

## How do I put a micro SD card in my phone?

On phones with a fixed battery, the card goes in the tray. These steps follow [Samsung UK support](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/using-an-sd-card/) and [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/).

1. Insert the ejector pin from the box into the hole on the side of the tray. Look for the tray outline so you don't push it into a microphone or speaker hole.
2. When the tray pops out, pull it out.
3. Follow the markings on the tray and place the card in the microSD space, gold contacts facing down.
4. Push the tray back in.

If the tray won't come out or gets damaged, Samsung says service may be needed. On models with a removable back cover and battery, turn the phone off and remove the cover and battery before inserting the card. Locations vary, so follow your manual.

## Portable or internal storage: which should I choose?

Choose portable storage if you plan to move the card. Here is the comparison from [Google's Android Help](https://support.google.com/android/answer/12153449?hl=en).

| Type | Photos and videos | Install apps | Move to another device |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Portable storage | Yes | No | Yes |
| Internal storage | Yes | Yes | No |

After you insert the card:

1. Swipe down from the top of the screen.
2. Tap the SD card detected notification.
3. For portable storage, select Format. For internal (adoptable) storage, select Format another way.
4. When formatting finishes, choose Move content or Move content later.

Formatting permanently deletes the data on the card. Google says to back up your files before you format. [Files by Google Help](https://support.google.com/files/answer/10857645?hl=en) also recommends portable storage.

Galaxy owners should know one more thing. [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) says Samsung Android devices don't support the Adoptable storage feature. The same page says the card must be FAT32 or exFAT, with exFAT preferred. FAT32 can't hold files over 4GB, and NTFS and APFS aren't compatible.

## How do I save camera photos straight to the card?

Change the storage location in the camera app. These are the Galaxy steps from [Samsung UK support](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/using-an-sd-card/).

1. Open the Camera app.
2. Tap the settings cog.
3. Tap Storage location.
4. Tap SD card.

The same page says you can't send everything on the phone to the card. Only some apps let you change where they save. Samsung also explains how to set Internet app downloads to the SD card under Settings > Sites and downloads. Menu names can vary by model and software version.

If you use Files by Google, turn on Save to SD card in the app's settings, following its [guide to saving files](https://support.google.com/files/answer/9321529?hl=en). This setting applies to files received with the app's peer-to-peer sharing.

## How do I move existing photos and files to the card?

Move or copy them in a file manager app.

| App | Steps | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Files by Google | Pick a category → find the file → More → Move to or Copy to → SD card → choose a folder → Move here or Copy here | [Google guide](https://support.google.com/files/answer/9712028?hl=en) |
| Galaxy My Files | Touch and hold the file → Move or Copy → My Files home → SD card → choose a folder → Move here or Copy here | [Samsung guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) |

Files by Google works on Android 8.0 and up. Samsung says this method only works in My Files, not in other apps such as Gallery.

Moving removes the original from the phone. Cards can be lost or fail too, so keep another copy of important photos on a computer or another storage device. To open the card on a computer, see [SD card reader for PC](/blog/en/sd-card-to-pc).

## Can I move apps to the micro SD card?

Only some apps. These steps follow [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/).

1. Open Settings and tap Apps.
2. Select the app you want to move.
3. Tap Storage, then Change.
4. Choose SD card and tap Move.

If Change doesn't appear, the app can't be moved. Samsung says many apps are blocked from the card by their developers, and preloaded apps can't be moved.

## How do I remove the card safely?

Unmount it first. [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) says you must unmount the card before removing it.

1. Open Settings, then tap Device care and Storage.
2. Tap More options (three dots), then Storage settings.
3. Tap Unmount for the SD card.
4. Use the ejector pin to pull out the tray and take out the card.
5. Put the empty tray back.

[Samsung UK support](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/using-an-sd-card/) also gives the path Settings > Device care > Storage > Advanced > SD card > Unmount. The menu path varies by model.

## What if my phone doesn't recognize the SD card?

Check in order.

| Step | What to check | What to do | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Storage in Settings | See if the card appears. If not, remove and reinsert it | [Files by Google guide](https://support.google.com/files/answer/10857645?hl=en) |
| 2 | Missing only in the app | If Settings shows it but the app doesn't, it may be formatted as internal storage. Reformat it as portable | Same guide |
| 3 | Card condition | Unmount, remove it, check for damage or debris, clean it, and reinsert | [Samsung UK guide](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/using-an-sd-card/) |
| 4 | Another device | Try it in another phone. If it works there, suspect the original phone | Same guide |

Reformatting erases the card. Before you do, try opening it on a computer and copying the files you need. More checks are in [SD card corrupted or not recognized?](/blog/en/sd-card-not-working), and reformatting on a computer is in [how to reformat a micro SD card](/blog/en/format-microsd).

## Can I use a micro SD card with an iPhone?

An iPhone has no card slot. According to [Apple's guide to external storage devices](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios), you connect a card reader to the charging port and open the card in the Files app. The drive must have a single data partition and be formatted as APFS, Mac OS Extended (HFS+), exFAT, FAT32, or FAT.

An exFAT card used as portable storage on an Android phone is in a format the iPhone can open. Connection steps are in [can you plug an SD card into an iPhone?](/blog/en/iphone-sd-card-slot) and [micro SD for iPhone](/blog/en/iphone-microsd).

## Micro SD card for phone FAQ

### Can I use an SD card as internal storage on a Galaxy phone?

Samsung US support says Samsung Android devices don't support Adoptable storage. Use the card as portable storage.

### Can a computer open a card formatted as internal storage?

Google's help says a card formatted as internal storage can't be moved between devices. If you want to use it on a computer, format it as portable storage.

### What happens if I encrypt the SD card on my phone?

According to Samsung US support, an encrypted card can only be read on the device that encrypted it. If you factory reset the phone while the card is encrypted, that phone can't read the card either. Decrypt it before resetting or using it in another device.

### Does a card add to my phone's storage?

A card formatted as portable storage gives you separate space for photos, videos, and files. Google's help says you can't install apps on portable storage. Apps save to the card only after you change their storage location, as with the camera.

### Can I move photos from the card back to the phone?

Yes. Open the SD card in the same file manager, select the files, and move or copy them to internal storage. The steps mirror moving files to the card.
