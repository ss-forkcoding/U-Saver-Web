## Key takeaways

How you use a micro SD card depends on the slot. Phones and game consoles take a microSD card directly. Computers and cameras with a full-size SD slot need an adapter.

An iPhone has no card slot. You connect a card reader with a microSD slot, or an adapter in an SD reader.

The order is the same on every device. Check that the device supports the card's standard, format it in the device you'll use, and eject it before you pull it out.

"How do I use a micro SD card?" "What do I need to read my phone's card on a computer?" These are common questions from first-time microSD users. The steps for a full-size SD card are in [how to use an SD card](/blog/en/sd-card-first-use). This guide covers microSD, device by device.

## How do I use a micro SD card on different devices?

The connection and the place you format it differ.

| Device | Where the card goes | Where to format | More help |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Android phone | The phone's microSD slot | Phone settings | The Android section below |
| Nintendo Switch | The console's microSD slot | Console settings | The Nintendo Switch section below |
| Mac or Windows PC | SD slot (with an adapter) or a card reader | The computer | [How to reformat a micro SD card](/blog/en/format-microsd) |
| iPhone | A card reader | Files app or a computer | [Micro SD for iPhone](/blog/en/iphone-microsd) |

Formatting erases everything on the card. If the card was used in another device, copy the files you need somewhere else first.

## Is a microSD card used differently from an SD card?

The size and the lock switch differ. According to the [SD Association FAQ](https://www.sdcard.org/consumers/faq/), microSD and SD cards differ only in form factor, size, number of pins, and whether they have a write-protect switch. The logic and electronics are identical.

| | microSD | SD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slot | microSD slot, or an SD slot with an adapter | SD slot |
| Write-protect switch | None on the card | On the side of the card |
| Capacity standards | microSD, microSDHC, microSDXC | SD, SDHC, SDXC |

When you put a microSD card in an adapter, check the lock switch on the side of the adapter. In the locked position, you can't save or delete files. See [SD card lock switch](/blog/en/sd-card-lock-switch). The differences between the two cards are in [micro SD card and SD card](/blog/en/microsd-vs-sd), and another name for the same card is covered in [what is a TF card?](/blog/en/tf-card)

## How do I use a micro SD card in an Android phone?

Insert the card, then choose a format type from the notification. These steps follow [Google's Android Help](https://support.google.com/android/answer/12153449?hl=en).

1. Insert the microSD card as shown in your phone's manual.
2. Swipe down from the top of the screen.
3. Tap the SD card detected notification.
4. For portable storage, select Format. For internal (adoptable) storage, select Format another way.

| Type | Photos and videos | Install apps | Move to another device |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Portable storage | Yes | No | Yes |
| Internal storage | Yes | Yes | No |

If you plan to move the card to a computer or another device, choose portable storage. [Files by Google Help](https://support.google.com/files/answer/10857645?hl=en) also recommends portable storage. Menu names can vary by phone maker. If you plan to install apps on the card, see [A2 SD card vs A1](/blog/en/sd-card-a1-a2).

## How do I move files to a micro SD card on Android?

Use the Files by Google app. According to [Files by Google's guide to moving files](https://support.google.com/files/answer/9712028?hl=en), the app works on Android 8.0 and up.

1. Open Files by Google.
2. Under Categories, select a category and find the file.
3. At the top right, tap More, then Move to or Copy to.
4. Tap SD card and choose a folder.
5. Tap Move here or Copy here.

If the card shows up in Settings but not in the app, it may be formatted as internal storage. The same guide says to reformat it as portable storage in that case. Formatting erases the card.

## How do I use a micro SD card in a Nintendo Switch?

The Switch and the Switch 2 take different cards. This table follows the [Nintendo microSD Card FAQ](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/22481/~/microsd-card-faq).

| Console | Supported cards |
| --- | --- |
| Nintendo Switch, Switch Lite, Switch OLED | microSD (up to 2 GB), microSDHC (4–32 GB), microSDXC (64 GB–2 TB) |
| Nintendo Switch 2 | microSD Express only |

- A Switch needs a system update before it can use a microSDXC card.
- SD and miniSD cards don't fit the slot.
- The card holds downloadable software, update data, DLC, screenshots, and captured videos.
- Game save data stays in system memory and can't be copied to the card.

Nintendo recommends using a single microSD card. Software saved across several cards can't be combined onto one card later. For microSD Express, see [what is microSD Express?](/blog/en/microsd-express)

## How do I use a micro SD card on a computer?

If the computer has an SD slot, put the card in an adapter. If not, use a card reader. [Apple's Mac SD card slot guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102352) says to use an adapter for smaller cards such as MiniSD and MicroSD.

1. Push the microSD card all the way into the adapter or the reader's microSD slot.
2. In a Mac SD slot, insert the card with the metal contacts facing down and pointing toward the computer. Don't force it.
3. Open the card in Finder or File Explorer and copy files.
4. When you're done, eject the card before removing it.

Apple warns that cards thicker than 2.1 mm might damage the slot. If the card doesn't mount, remove it and insert it again. To pick a reader, see [how to choose an SD card reader](/blog/en/choose-sd-card-reader).

## How do I remove a micro SD card safely?

Eject first, then remove the card.

1. On a Mac, drag the card's icon to the Trash. [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102352) says removing a card while the Mac is asleep could lead to data loss.
2. On Windows, use the Safely Remove Hardware icon on the taskbar to eject the card. [Microsoft's guide](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/safely-remove-hardware-in-windows-1ee6677d-4e6c-4359-efca-fd44b9cec369) says to do this before removing a device to avoid losing data.
3. On phones and game consoles, follow the card removal steps in the device's manual.

## How do I use a micro SD card with an iPhone?

Connect a card reader to the charging port. According to [Apple's guide to external storage](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios), the device must have a single data partition. It must be formatted as APFS, macOS Extended (HFS+), exFAT, FAT32, or FAT.

1. Insert the microSD card into the reader. Apple says the card fits only one way, so don't force it.
2. Connect the reader to your iPhone's charging port.
3. In the Files app, tap Browse, then tap the card's name under Locations.

You can import photos and videos from the card directly into the Photos app. Reader types and import steps are in [micro SD for iPhone](/blog/en/iphone-microsd). If a card from an Android phone doesn't appear, see [how to reformat a micro SD card](/blog/en/format-microsd).

## What if my micro SD card isn't recognized?

Check these in order.

1. Remove the card and insert it again.
2. Check that the device supports the card's capacity standard. Some devices, such as the Switch 2, take only microSD Express.
3. If you use an adapter, check that its lock switch is in the unlocked position.
4. On Android, check whether the card appears under Storage in Settings. If it doesn't, reinsert the card.
5. If it still fails, contact the card maker or the seller. The [SD Association FAQ](https://www.sdcard.org/consumers/faq/) also directs product issues to the manufacturer.

More checks are in [SD card corrupted or not recognized?](/blog/en/sd-card-not-working)

## FAQ: how do I use a micro SD card

### Can I put a microSD card straight into an SD slot?

No. Apple says to use an adapter for smaller cards such as MicroSD in an SD slot.

### Can I open my phone's microSD card on a computer?

Yes, if it's formatted as portable storage. Google's help says a card formatted as internal storage can't be moved between devices.

### Can I use my Nintendo Switch microSD card in a Switch 2?

According to Nintendo's FAQ, the Switch 2 takes only microSD Express cards. A regular microSD card can't be used in a Switch 2.

### Does a microSD card have a lock switch?

No. The SD Association FAQ lists the write-protect switch as one of the differences between microSD and SD. An adapter may have one.

### How long does a microSD card last?

The SD Association FAQ says normal use typically gives a card a lifespan of 10 years or more. It varies by product and use, so keep a second copy of important photos on another storage device.
