## Key takeaways

Picking a 3DS SD card comes down to two checks. Match the card size to your model, and choose an SDHC card of 32 GB or less. Nintendo says the 3DS family supports cards up to 32 GB and doesn't support SDXC cards of 64 GB or more.

The Nintendo 3DS, 3DS XL, and 2DS take full-size SD cards. The New Nintendo 3DS, New 3DS XL, and New 2DS XL take microSD cards.

To move to a new card, copy the whole "Nintendo 3DS" folder with a computer. Don't pick out or change files inside it.

"I put a 64 GB card in my 3DS and it won't read it." "Where does the card go on a New 3DS?" These questions come up when people dig an old 3DS out of a drawer. This guide covers which 3DS SD card works, how to insert and format it, and how to move to a bigger card, using Nintendo's own support pages. It hasn't tested any card and doesn't recommend one. For the Switch, see the [Nintendo Switch micro SD card guide](/blog/en/switch-microsd).

## Which 3DS SD card should I use?

Use the right card size for your model, at 32 GB or less. This table combines Nintendo's [3DS card compatibility page](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/274/~/what-sd-cards-and-microsd-cards-are-compatible) and [Nintendo Japan's maximum capacity Q&A](https://support-jp.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/31955/).

| Model | Card size | Supported cards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nintendo 3DS, 3DS XL, 2DS | SD | SD up to 2 GB, SDHC 4 GB–32 GB |
| New Nintendo 3DS, New 3DS XL, New 2DS XL | microSD | microSD up to 2 GB, microSDHC 4 GB–32 GB |
| All 3DS family systems | - | SDXC and microSDXC are not considered compatible |

Nintendo says it can't guarantee that cards other than the one included with the system will work. Nintendo Japan lists some third-party cards it has checked, but says that list isn't a guarantee either. The list was last updated in December 2014.

The SDHC and SDXC marks on a card are capacity standards. They're explained in [SDHC vs SDXC vs SDUC](/blog/en/sdhc-vs-sdxc). If you doubt a new card's real capacity, check it first with [how to spot a fake SD card](/blog/en/fake-sd-card).

## Can a 3DS use a 64 GB SD card?

Not officially. A 64 GB card is an SDXC card, and Nintendo lists SDXC and microSDXC as not compatible with the 3DS family. Nintendo Japan's Q&A also gives 32 GB as the maximum.

If you're running out of space, move to a 32 GB card and delete software you don't use. [Nintendo's SD card usage page](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/399/~/what-are-sd-cards-and-microsd-cards-used-for%3F) says one card can hold up to 300 software titles, 3,000 photos, and 3,000 music files.

## What does a 3DS SD card store?

Downloaded software, photos, and music. This follows Nintendo's SD card usage page.

| Data | Where it's stored |
| --- | --- |
| Downloaded software (except DSiWare) | SD card |
| Photos taken with Nintendo 3DS Camera | Can be saved to the SD card |
| Music files | Played from the SD card |
| Game progress for Game Card software | The Game Card |

[Nintendo's save location page](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/337/) says game progress for Game Card software is saved to the Game Card. It adds that some Game Cards save StreetPass and SpotPass information to the SD card or system memory. Nintendo also notes that files in folders more than eight levels deep won't be displayed.

## How do I insert an SD card into a 3DS?

Turn the system off first. [Nintendo's card insertion page](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/271/) says the system must be powered off before you insert or remove a card.

| Model | Slot location | Card direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nintendo 3DS | Left side, under the slot cover | Label side down |
| Nintendo 3DS XL, 2DS | Right side, under the slot cover | Label side down |
| New Nintendo 2DS XL | Front, under the slot cover | Label side down |
| New Nintendo 3DS, New 3DS XL | Under the battery cover | Facing away from the system |

1. Turn the system off.
2. Open the slot cover or battery cover shown in the table.
3. Push the card in until it clicks.
4. Close the cover and turn the system on.

[Nintendo's card transfer page](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/309/~/how-to-transfer-data-between-sd-cards-or-microsd-cards) says you need a size #0 crosshead screwdriver to remove the microSD card from a New 3DS or New 3DS XL.

When the write-protection switch on an SD card is down, you can't save or erase data. Keep it up. See [the SD card lock switch](/blog/en/sd-card-lock-switch) for details.

## How do I format a 3DS SD card?

Use the SD Association's SD Memory Card Formatter on a computer. [Nintendo's 3DS formatting page](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/220/~/how-to-format-an-sd-card-or-microsd-card) recommends this tool for any card used with a Nintendo product and says to select Overwrite format.

1. Copy the card's photos and music to a computer. Formatting erases everything on the card.
2. Put the card in the computer's slot or a card reader.
3. Open SD Memory Card Formatter and choose the card.
4. Select Overwrite format and start formatting.

The tool's screens and the difference from Quick format are in [how to use SD Memory Card Formatter](/blog/en/sd-card-formatter). Connecting a card to a computer is covered in [SD card reader for PC](/blog/en/sd-card-to-pc).

## How do I move to a bigger 3DS SD card?

Copy the whole card with a computer. These steps follow [Nintendo's card transfer page](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/309/~/how-to-transfer-data-between-sd-cards-or-microsd-cards), which covers both Windows Explorer and the Mac Finder.

1. Turn the system off and remove the old card.
2. Put the old card in the computer's slot or a card reader. A microSD card may need an adapter.
3. Open the card in Explorer or Finder and drag all of its folders to the desktop.
4. Format the new card the way Nintendo recommends.
5. Drag the folders from the desktop to the new card.
6. Put the new card in the system and check that it works.

| Watch out for | What Nintendo says |
| --- | --- |
| The Nintendo 3DS folder | Don't click into it to pick files. Copy the entire folder and never alter or erase the files inside |
| The old card | Don't delete, format, or alter it until the new card works |
| After the move | Don't use save data from the old card |
| Several cards | Don't swap between cards. Use one card with one system |
| Another system | Copied content won't work on another system. Use System Transfer to move to a new system |

The same page says New 3DS family systems can also reach card data over your home network without removing the card.

## Can I redownload deleted 3DS software?

Yes, for software you bought. [Nintendo's 3DS eShop discontinuation Q&A](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/57847/~/wii-u-&-nintendo-3ds-eshop-discontinuation-q&a) says purchases in Nintendo eShop for the 3DS family ended on March 27, 2023 at 5:00 PM Pacific Time. It also says that for the foreseeable future you can still redownload games and DLC and get software updates.

You can't buy anything new. Free content such as demos also stopped being available on the same date. Keep that in mind before you format or replace a card.

## How do I move 3DS photos to an iPhone?

Send the photos to the SD card in the Nintendo 3DS Camera, then connect the card to the iPhone with a card reader. These steps follow [Nintendo's photo transfer page](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/113/).

1. In Nintendo 3DS Camera, select View Photos.
2. Select Settings, then Data Management.
3. Tap System to SD Card. You can't pick single photos; all photos move at once.
4. Turn the system off, remove the card, and put it in a card reader.

[Apple's external storage guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios) says the iPhone reads formats including exFAT, FAT32, and FAT. This guide hasn't tested a 3DS card on an iPhone. Copy only the photos, and don't change or delete files in the Nintendo 3DS folder. Connection steps are in [how to use an iPhone SD card reader](/blog/en/iphone-sd-card-reader), and moving iPhone photos to a card is in [transferring photos from iPhone to SD card](/blog/en/iphone-photos-to-sd-card).

## What if my 3DS doesn't recognize the SD card?

These steps follow [Nintendo's SD card troubleshooting page](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/452/~/general-sd-card-and-microsd-card-troubleshooting).

1. Turn the system off and remove the card.
2. Check that the card is compatible. SDXC cards of 64 GB or more aren't.
3. Make sure the SD card's lock switch is unlocked. microSD cards don't have a lock switch.
4. Insert the card and turn the system on.
5. If a message says the card may be full, delete unused content.
6. If it still fails, try another card or another system. If another card works, replace the card.

The same page says not to remove a microSD card while its adapter is still in the system. Nintendo of America no longer services 3DS family systems other than the New 2DS XL. Checking the card itself is covered in [SD card corrupted or not recognized?](/blog/en/sd-card-not-working)

## 3DS SD card FAQ

### What's the largest SD card a 3DS supports?

32 GB. Nintendo lists SD cards up to 2 GB and SDHC cards from 4 GB to 32 GB. SDXC cards of 64 GB or more are not considered compatible.

### Can I put a regular SD card in a New 3DS?

No. The New 3DS, New 3DS XL, and New 2DS XL use microSD cards. Nintendo Japan's table also marks full-size SD cards as unsupported on the New 3DS family. Size differences are in [micro SD card and SD card](/blog/en/microsd-vs-sd).

### Can an original 3DS use a microSD card?

Yes, with an SD card adapter. Nintendo says miniSD and microSD cards work in the 3DS, 3DS XL, and 2DS with an adapter.

### Does formatting the SD card erase Game Card progress?

Game progress for Game Card software is saved to the Game Card. Downloaded software, photos, and music on the SD card are erased. Copy the card to a computer before you format it.

### Can I swap between several SD cards?

Nintendo says to use one card with one system. If you need more space, move everything to a bigger card with the steps above.
