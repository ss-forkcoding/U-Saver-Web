## Key takeaways

Canceling iCloud+ moves you back to the free 5 GB plan. The change takes effect after your current billing period ends.

If what you store in iCloud is more than 5 GB, iCloud stops syncing and iCloud backups won't complete after you cancel. That's why Apple tells you to download or remove content that exceeds your new storage amount before you cancel.

You can copy photos, videos, files, and contacts to external storage such as a USB drive. Messages and iPhone settings have no built-in way to be saved as files. To keep those too, use a computer backup.

"I want to stop paying for iCloud every month." "If I cancel iCloud+, do my photos disappear?" "Can I keep my photos somewhere without a computer?" People ask these questions when they want to live without an iCloud subscription. This guide covers what to move before you cancel and how to cancel. If you'd rather keep your plan and free up space, see [What to do when iCloud storage is full](/blog/en/icloud-storage-full).

## What changes when you cancel iCloud+?

Here is what [Apple's guide to downgrading or canceling iCloud+](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108318) says.

| Item | After you cancel |
| --- | --- |
| When it applies | After your current subscription billing period ends |
| Storage | You move to the free 5 GB plan |
| If you're over 5 GB | iCloud won't sync or update your information, and iCloud backups won't complete |
| When syncing resumes | When you increase storage or delete information to make space |
| iCloud+ features | You lose features such as Hide My Email, Private Relay, and HomeKit Secure Video support |
| Refunds | Partial refunds are available where required by law. If you recently changed your plan, contact Apple within 14 days |

Apple's guide doesn't say that canceling deletes the photos already in iCloud. But new photos stop uploading, and backups stop too. If you lose your iPhone after that, anything new isn't saved anywhere else.

## What should you move before you cancel iCloud+?

First, check your usage in Settings > [your name] > iCloud. You need to move or delete whatever is over 5 GB. This table follows [Apple's guide to copying what you store in iCloud](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108306).

| Data | Where to move it with only an iPhone | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photos and videos | External storage | Export Unmodified Original in Photos |
| iCloud Drive files | External storage or On My iPhone | Copy Items in Files, then Paste |
| Contacts | Export to a file | See [How to back up contacts on iPhone](/blog/en/iphone-contacts-backup) |
| Messages, iPhone settings | No built-in way | Use a computer backup |

If most of your space is photos and videos, one USB drive can cover it. For a comparison of ways to back up without a computer, see [Back up your iPhone without a computer](/blog/en/backup-iphone-without-computer).

## How do you copy photos to a USB drive before canceling?

With iCloud Photos on, your iPhone may only hold smaller versions. Download the originals to your iPhone first. These steps follow [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108306).

1. Connect the USB drive to your iPhone.
2. Open the Settings app and tap your name.
3. Tap iCloud, then tap Photos.
4. Under iPhone Storage, choose Download and Keep Originals.
5. In the Photos app, go to your library, tap Select, and choose the photos and videos you want to copy.
6. Tap the Share button, then tap Export Unmodified Original.
7. Under Locations, tap the USB drive, then tap Save.

Downloading originals takes time, so stay on Wi-Fi. When the copy finishes, open the drive in the Files app and open a few photos and one video to check them. For screen-by-screen steps, see [How to move iPhone photos to a USB drive](/blog/en/iphone-photos-to-usb).

If the drive is FAT32, videos over 4 GB won't save. According to [Microsoft's file system comparison](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/fileio/filesystem-functionality-comparison), the maximum file size on FAT32 is 4 GiB. If you have long videos, prepare the drive with [How to format a USB drive to exFAT](/blog/en/format-usb-exfat). NTFS isn't on the list of supported formats in [Apple's external storage guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios).

## How do you copy iCloud Drive files?

These steps follow [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108306).

1. Open the Files app and tap Browse.
2. Under Locations, tap iCloud Drive, then tap the More button.
3. Tap Select, then choose the files and folders you want to copy.
4. Tap More, then tap Copy Items.
5. Under Locations, open the USB drive, touch and hold a folder, then tap Paste.

Files optimized in iCloud Drive download to your iPhone before they're copied. If you copy a file from a shared folder, the sharing access doesn't come with it.

## What won't you keep after canceling iCloud+?

[Apple's guide to backup methods](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108771) lists two ways to back up an iPhone: iCloud and your computer. An iCloud backup includes the information and settings stored on your device.

| Data | How to keep it without iCloud |
| --- | --- |
| Photos, videos, files | Copy them to external storage such as a USB drive |
| Contacts | Export them to a contacts file |
| Messages, iPhone settings, app data | Use a computer backup |

If you cancel while you're over 5 GB, iCloud backups won't complete. To protect messages and settings, either shrink your iCloud backup below 5 GB or make a computer backup. If your backup has already stopped, see [iPhone not backing up](/blog/en/iphone-backup-fails).

## How do you cancel iCloud+?

Once your data is copied, cancel the plan. These are the steps for iOS 18.4 and later in [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108318).

1. Open the Settings app and tap your name.
2. Tap Subscriptions, then tap iCloud+ under Active.
3. Tap Cancel Subscription. You'll see a message saying that you may not have enough iCloud storage to sync all your data.

To move to a smaller plan instead, tap See All Plans in step 3 and choose a new plan. Plan prices are in [the iCloud storage guide](/blog/en/icloud-storage-full). If you no longer have an Apple device, use the online cancellation method linked from Apple's guide.

## If you copy photos to a USB drive regularly

After you cancel, new photos no longer upload to iCloud. Exporting from the Photos app means choosing photos and a save location every time. Set a regular day to copy new photos to the drive.

## FAQ: keeping iPhone data without an iCloud subscription

### Does canceling iCloud+ take effect right away?

No. According to Apple, a downgrade or cancellation takes effect after your current billing period ends. You have time to copy your data first.

### Will canceling iCloud+ delete my photos?

Apple says that if you're over your storage, syncing, updates, and backups stop. It doesn't say your photos are deleted. New photos won't upload, though, so make a copy on a USB drive before you cancel.

### What happens if I'm over 5 GB after canceling?

iCloud won't sync or update your information, and iCloud backups won't complete. Both resume when you add storage or delete information to make space.

### Can I back up my iPhone without a computer or iCloud?

You can copy photos, videos, files, and contacts to a USB drive. Messages and iPhone settings need one of Apple's backup methods: iCloud or a computer.

### Can I get a refund after canceling?

Apple says partial refunds are available where required by law. If you recently changed your plan, you can ask Apple for a refund within 14 days.
