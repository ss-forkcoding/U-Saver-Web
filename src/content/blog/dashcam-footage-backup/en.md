## Key takeaways

Dash cam footage doesn't stay on the card for long. When the card fills up, the dash cam deletes the oldest unsaved video and keeps recording. Save the clips you need, then copy them off the card.

If a video seems to be missing, stop recording on that card. Don't format it, even if your computer asks you to. Copy whatever files it can still read first.

For getting deleted files back, the SD Association points you to the card maker's instructions. This guide doesn't recommend or test any recovery software, and it can't promise that lost footage will come back.

"How do I get the accident footage off my dash cam?" "Yesterday's video isn't on the card." This guide explains why dash cam footage disappears and how to copy it off safely. For choosing and formatting the card, see [micro SD card for dash cam](/blog/en/dashcam-sd-card). The models below are only examples of how to read a manual.

## Why does dash cam footage disappear?

A dash cam starts recording when the car starts and overwrites the card when it's full. Footage disappears in four common ways.

| Case | What happens | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The card is full | The oldest unsaved video is deleted automatically | [Garmin manual](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/dashcam45-55/EN-US/GUID-39BB7445-FF9C-4CC2-89AF-07311CAF07C9.html) |
| "Promptly delete" is on | Unsaved video more than three minutes old is deleted continually, and all unsaved video is deleted each time the camera powers off | [Garmin 46/56/66W camera settings](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/dashcam46-56-66w/EN-US/GUID-59420DC0-4EBA-400F-9B2F-0DBD0C3FC266.html) |
| The card is formatted | All footage is erased, including locked event files | [BlackVue manual](https://manual.blackvue.com/docs/dr770x-series-ii/system-updates-and-sd-card-maintenance/formatting-the-microsd-card-2/) |
| The card fails | The camera can look like it's recording while nothing is saved | [Japan's National Consumer Affairs Center](https://www.kokusen.go.jp/news/data/n-20180802_2.html) |

Garmin says the promptly delete option exists only in some regions and is on by default in some of them. Check when your dash cam deletes unsaved video.

In the Center's tests with a card that couldn't be read or written, three products didn't detect the problem. The Center notes this is one example under specific conditions and advises checking recorded footage regularly.

## How do you keep dash cam footage from being overwritten?

Save the clip. The Garmin manual says saving a recording prevents it from being overwritten or deleted. How you save depends on the model, so check your manual. Here is how two manuals describe it.

| Method | Garmin 46/56/66W | BlackVue DR770X Series II |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Automatic save on impact | Saves footage before, during, and after a detected incident | Event recordings start 5 seconds before the event |
| Manual save | Hold the button to save footage before, during, and after you hold it | With the touch sensor enabled, touch it to start a manual recording |
| Save earlier footage | Gallery > Unsaved Videos, pick a time, then save | Not found in the manual |

Garmin details come from the [Garmin 46/56/66W owner's manual (PDF)](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/dashcam46-56-66w/EN-US/Dash_Cam_46-56-66W_OM_EN-US.pdf). BlackVue details come from the [BlackVue app guide](https://manual.blackvue.com/docs/dr770x-series-ii/viewing-and-managing-videos/%f0%9f%93%b1-using-the-blackvue-app-3/).

A saved clip is still on the card. The Garmin manual says the card has limited storage, so you should transfer saved recordings to a computer or smartphone. The same manual says the device stops recording while you view videos or pictures. Don't open the gallery while driving.

## How do you get dash cam footage off the card?

There are three ways. Check which ones your dash cam supports.

| Method | When to use it | Manual example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remove the card and connect it to a computer | Moving many clips at once | Garmin says you can connect the memory card or the device to a computer to transfer videos |
| Connect the dash cam to a computer with a data cable | When the card is hard to remove | The Garmin 46/56/66W uses the shorter included data cable; the longer power cable can't connect to a computer |
| Download to a phone with the maker's app | Moving one or two clips | The BlackVue app downloads clips with "Download to my device" while connected to the dash cam's Wi-Fi |

The data cable note is in [Garmin's guide to connecting the camera to a computer](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/dashcam46-56-66w/EN-US/GUID-8839611D-E8FB-4848-9289-E1AC71DD302A.html). Once connected, the camera appears as a removable drive.

## How do you copy dash cam footage to a computer?

Per the [Garmin 46/56/66W guide](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/dashcam46-56-66w/EN-US/GUID-E75CB7BE-ACAB-4427-A608-A4D9F919A911.html), videos are stored as MP4 files in the DCIM folder on the card. Here are the steps, using that model as the example.

1. Put the card in a reader and connect it to your computer, or connect the dash cam with its data cable.
2. Open the card drive in File Explorer on Windows or Finder on a Mac.
3. In the DCIM folder, find the folder you need. Garmin sorts footage into 100EVENT (incidents), 102SAVED (saved manually), 103PARKM (parked), and 105UNSVD (unsaved).
4. Copy the video files to a folder on your computer. Don't move or delete files on the card.
5. Play the copied clips on your computer.
6. Eject the card before you remove it.

Folder names differ by model. Garmin notes that some media players might not support high-resolution playback. If a clip won't play, try the maker's viewer or another player.

[Microsoft says](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/safely-remove-hardware-in-windows-1ee6677d-4e6c-4359-efca-fd44b9cec369) to always safely remove USB devices before disconnecting them to avoid losing data. For connecting and ejecting a card, see [SD card reader for PC](/blog/en/sd-card-to-pc) and [SD card reader for Mac](/blog/en/sd-card-on-mac).

## What should you do first when dash cam footage is missing?

Stop anything that could erase more. Go through these steps in order.

1. Stop recording on that card. A dash cam overwrites unsaved video when the card fills up.
2. Connect the card to a computer and copy any files it can read to another drive.
3. If the computer asks you to format the card, don't. Formatting erases all footage.
4. If the card isn't recognized, check the reader, the contacts, and the lock switch on the SD adapter. For the steps, see [SD card corrupted or not recognized](/blog/en/sd-card-not-working).
5. For deleted files, check the card maker's instructions.

The [SD Association FAQ](https://www.sdcard.org/consumers/faq/) says to refer to your card maker's instructions to retrieve deleted pictures and that the SD Association doesn't supply retrieval tools. Footage that's already overwritten, or deleted by a promptly delete setting, may not come back. If the footage matters, such as after an accident, keep the card as it is and contact the dash cam or card maker first.

## Where should you keep dash cam footage you've copied?

The dash cam will overwrite its card again, and the Consumer Affairs Center calls SD cards consumables. Keep important footage off the card.

| Where | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A folder on your computer | Put the date and what happened in the folder name |
| An external drive, such as a USB flash drive | Copy it to a second place in case the computer fails |
| Your phone | Clips downloaded with the app appear in the phone's gallery or in the app's download list (BlackVue app) |

Before the card goes back into the dash cam, make sure the copies play. For formatting intervals and steps, see [micro SD card for dash cam](/blog/en/dashcam-sd-card) and [how to reformat a micro SD card](/blog/en/format-microsd).

## How do you move dash cam footage to an iPhone?

iPhone has no card slot, so use an SD card reader. Per [Apple's external storage guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios), a storage device must have a single data partition and use a format such as exFAT or FAT32. In the Files app, tap Browse, then tap the card's name under Locations.

## FAQ: dash cam footage

### Can overwritten dash cam footage be recovered?

We couldn't confirm a method. The SD Association points to the card maker's instructions for retrieving deleted files. If the footage matters, stop using the card and contact the maker.

### Do event files survive formatting?

No. The BlackVue manual says formatting erases all data, including locked event files. Copy them to a computer first.

### Is footage deleted when I turn off the car?

By default, the oldest unsaved video is deleted when the card is full. With Garmin's promptly delete option on, all unsaved video is deleted each time the camera powers off. Check your dash cam's settings.

### Can I watch footage on the dash cam screen?

The Garmin manual says the device stops recording while you view videos. Watch only when parked, and check that recording resumes afterward.

### My computer asks me to format the card. What now?

Don't format it. Formatting erases the footage. Follow the checks in [SD card corrupted or not recognized](/blog/en/sd-card-not-working) first.
