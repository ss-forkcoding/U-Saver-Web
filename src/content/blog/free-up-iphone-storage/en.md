## Key takeaways

If you're asking why your iPhone storage is full when you have iCloud, the short answer is that they're two different spaces. Upgrading your iCloud plan doesn't add any storage to your iPhone.

Even with iCloud, original photos, downloaded iCloud Drive files, and old messages can stay on your iPhone. A few settings reduce how much space they take.

If that isn't enough, move photos and videos to another storage device such as a USB drive, then delete them. Check that the copies open before you delete anything.

"I pay for 200 GB of iCloud, so why is my iPhone full?" "Everything is in iCloud, so can I just delete it from my iPhone?" These questions come up often. The basic steps for checking storage are in [iPhone storage full? What to do first](/blog/en/iphone-storage-full). This guide covers the settings to change if you use iCloud.

## Why is iPhone storage full when I have iCloud?

[Apple's guide to iCloud storage and device storage](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102670) explains that they're separate. Your device comes with a set amount of storage.

| Type | What it is | Where to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iCloud storage | Online storage tied to your Apple Account | Settings > [your name] > iCloud |
| Device storage | Storage inside your iPhone | Settings > General > iPhone Storage |

Buying more iCloud storage only increases iCloud storage. A "Storage Almost Full" alert on your iPhone means device storage is low. If your iCloud storage is the one that's full, see [what to do when iCloud storage is full](/blog/en/icloud-storage-full).

## What uses iPhone storage even when it's in iCloud?

According to Apple's guides, some content in iCloud also stays on your iPhone.

| Item | Why it stays on your iPhone | Setting to change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iCloud Photos | [Apple says](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108782) photos and videos in iCloud Photos use both your iCloud storage and your device storage | Optimize iPhone Storage |
| iCloud Drive | [Apple says](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108429) iCloud Drive content you downloaded counts toward device storage | Remove Download |
| Messages | Messages and their attachments count toward iPhone storage | Keep Messages |
| New photos and videos | File size depends on the camera format | High Efficiency camera format |
| Apps you rarely use | Installed apps keep using space | Offload Unused Apps |

Each one is explained below. Before you change anything, open Settings > General > iPhone Storage to see which category is largest. Start with the largest one.

## How do you turn on Optimize iPhone Storage?

If Photos is large and you use iCloud Photos, check this setting first. According to [Apple's iCloud Photos guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108782), your iPhone keeps space-saving versions of photos and videos, while iCloud keeps the originals.

1. Open Settings and tap your name.
2. Tap iCloud.
3. Under Saved to iCloud, tap Photos.
4. Tap iPhone Storage, then choose Optimize iPhone Storage. In iOS 26 or earlier, choose Optimize iPhone Storage directly on the Photos screen.

Apple says that when you need more device storage, iCloud Photos optimizes your library starting with the photos and videos you access least. So you might not see the space free up right away. You can download originals over Wi-Fi or cellular.

## Can I delete photos from my iPhone if they're in iCloud Photos?

No. According to [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108782), when you delete photos and videos on one device, they're deleted everywhere you use iCloud Photos. If you delete them to free up iPhone storage, the originals in iCloud are gone too.

Apple says you can recover deleted items from Recently Deleted for 30 days, after which they're deleted permanently. Apple also says iCloud Photos aren't duplicated in your iCloud backup, so keep backup copies of your library.

To delete photos from your iPhone, first make a copy on another storage device. The steps for a USB drive are in [how to transfer photos from iPhone to USB](/blog/en/iphone-photos-to-usb).

## Can you free up space from iCloud Drive without deleting files?

Yes. According to [Apple's Files app guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104953), Remove Download takes a file off your iPhone without deleting it from iCloud Drive. The file stays in iCloud Drive, and you can download it again any time.

1. Open the Files app and tap the Browse tab.
2. Tap iCloud Drive.
3. Tap the More button, tap Select, then choose the downloaded files.
4. Tap the More button, then tap Remove Download.

For a single file, touch and hold the file, then tap Remove Download.

Don't confuse this with Delete. Apple says that if you delete files from iCloud Drive on one device, they're removed from every device signed in with the same Apple Account. Deleted files stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

| Option | On your iPhone | In iCloud Drive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remove Download | Removed | Stays |
| Delete | Deleted | Deleted from every device |

## How do you delete old messages automatically?

If Messages is large, change how long messages are kept. These steps follow [Apple's guide to deleting messages](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/delete-messages-and-attachments-iph2c9c4bfcb/ios).

1. Open the Settings app.
2. Tap Apps, then tap Messages.
3. Scroll down and tap Keep Messages, below Message History.
4. Choose 30 Days, 1 Year, or Forever.

If you choose an option other than Forever, conversations and all their attachments are removed automatically after that period. If you use Messages in iCloud, deleting a message on your iPhone deletes it from all your devices where Messages in iCloud is turned on.

If a conversation has a photo you want to keep, save it to the Photos app before you change this setting.

## Does changing the camera format save space?

It reduces the size of photos and videos you take from now on. According to [Apple's HEIF and HEVC guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/116944), HEIF (photos) and HEVC (videos) compress better than JPEG and H.264, so they use less storage while keeping the same visual quality.

1. Open Settings, then tap Camera.
2. Tap Formats.
3. Choose High Efficiency.

If you choose Most Compatible, new photos and videos use JPEG or H.264. Photos you've already taken don't change. Apple lists iPhone 7 and later as able to capture HEIF and HEVC.

## What about apps you rarely use?

According to the [iPhone User Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/manage-storage-on-iphone-iph47c931112/ios), your iPhone can remove unused apps automatically when storage is low while keeping your data.

1. Open the Settings app.
2. Tap Apps, then tap App Store.
3. Turn on Offload Unused Apps.

Offloaded apps stay on the Home Screen with a cloud symbol, and tapping one reinstalls it. To offload apps one at a time, see [iPhone storage full? What to do first](/blog/en/iphone-storage-full).

## What should you move if it's still full?

Move photos and videos to another storage device, then delete them from your iPhone. These steps save originals to a USB drive, following [Apple's guide to downloading photos](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111762).

1. Connect your iPhone to Wi-Fi and plug in the USB drive.
2. In the Photos app, go to your Library.
3. Tap Select, then choose the photos and videos to move.
4. Tap the Share button, then tap Export Unmodified Originals.
5. Choose the USB drive in the Files app, then tap Save.

With Optimize iPhone Storage on, the originals may not be on your iPhone, so downloading them takes time. When it finishes, open the USB drive in the Files app and open a few photos and one video. Screen-by-screen steps are in [how to transfer photos from iPhone to USB](/blog/en/iphone-photos-to-usb).

If the drive is FAT32, videos over 4 GB won't save. [Microsoft's file system comparison](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/fileio/filesystem-functionality-comparison) lists 4 GiB as the maximum file size for FAT32. If you have long videos, prepare the drive with [how to format a USB drive to exFAT](/blog/en/format-usb-exfat) and see [how to transfer video from iPhone to USB](/blog/en/iphone-videos-to-usb).

If you plan to downgrade or cancel your iCloud plan, check what to move first in [keeping iPhone photos without an iCloud subscription](/blog/en/without-icloud).

## FAQ: iPhone storage full when you have iCloud

### Does a bigger iCloud plan give my iPhone more storage?

No. According to Apple, iCloud storage and device storage are separate. You free up iPhone storage with settings such as Optimize iPhone Storage and Remove Download, or by moving and deleting content.

### I turned on Optimize iPhone Storage, but nothing changed.

Apple says iCloud Photos optimizes your library when you need more device storage, starting with the photos and videos you access least. It doesn't shrink every photo the moment you turn it on.

### What's the difference between Remove Download and Delete?

Remove Download takes the file off your iPhone and keeps it in iCloud Drive. Delete removes the file from every device signed in with the same Apple Account.

### What gets deleted if I set Keep Messages to 30 Days?

According to Apple, conversations and their attachments older than 30 days are removed automatically. If you use Messages in iCloud, they're removed from your other devices too.

### Does High Efficiency make my existing photos smaller?

No. It applies to new photos and videos you take after changing the setting. For existing photos, use Optimize iPhone Storage or move them to another storage device.
