## Key takeaways

A Fire tablet memory card is a microSD card. Amazon supports microSD on compatible Fire tablets from the 5th generation on. The maximum size runs from 200 GB on the 5th and 6th generations to 1 TB on the 10th generation and later.

When you first insert the card, a Fire tablet asks whether to use it as portable or internal storage. A card set up as internal storage can't be used in another device until you reformat it and erase everything on it.

Galaxy Tab models work differently. Samsung says its Android devices don't support adoptable storage, so the card always shows up as separate storage.

"How big a card can my Fire tablet take?" "It's asking me to pick portable or internal. Which one?" These are the questions people ask the first time they put a microSD card in a tablet. This guide covers tablets. For phones, see [how to use a microSD card in an Android phone](/blog/en/android-microsd) and [Galaxy SD card slot and settings](/blog/en/galaxy-sd-card).

## What size Fire tablet memory card can I use?

It depends on the generation. This table comes from [Amazon's Fire tablet microSD troubleshooting page](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GN5X9QEFQHZHZXLU).

| Fire tablet generation | Maximum card size |
| --- | --- |
| 5th and 6th generation | Up to 200 GB |
| 7th generation | Up to 256 GB |
| 8th and 9th generation | Up to 512 GB |
| 10th generation and later | Up to 1 TB |

The same page says the card should be UHS, or Class 4 or higher if it isn't UHS. On the Fire Max 11, you insert and remove the card through an eject pin hole. Speed markings are explained in [SD card speed classes](/blog/en/sd-card-speed-class). This guide hasn't tested any card and doesn't recommend one.

## Portable or internal storage on a Fire tablet?

Pick portable if you want to move files to other devices. According to [Amazon's tips for using microSD cards with Fire tablets](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GMRDCX9BUV3L7TKA), you choose the mode the first time you insert the card. The same page says Fire tablets only support the FAT32 file system.

| Mode | Best for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portable storage | Moving files, photos, and videos between devices | Some copy-protected movies and music may not transfer to other devices |
| Internal storage | Expanding the tablet's storage | The card is formatted and can't be used in another device until you reformat it and erase all data |

Amazon notes that the Kindle and Prime Video apps only support portable storage. If you pick internal storage, the tablet asks whether to move files to the card right away. If you skip that step, your content stays on the tablet. You can change these options later in the Fire tablet's Settings.

Amazon's pages differ on apps. The tips page says internal storage is meant for apps and games. The troubleshooting page says Fire tablets running Fire OS 6 or later can't install apps or games on a microSD card. If you want apps on the card, check the storage settings on your own model first.

## How do I remove a microSD card from a Fire tablet?

It depends on the mode. These steps come from [Amazon's microSD tips page](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GMRDCX9BUV3L7TKA).

| Mode | How to remove | What happens if you just pull it out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portable storage | Go to Settings > Storage and remove it safely | You may lose data |
| Internal storage | The card isn't meant to be removed | A message keeps popping up until you reinsert the card or forget it |

If you choose to forget an internal storage card, Amazon says everything stored on it, such as movies, books, and apps, is lost for good. App data on the card isn't available when you reinstall those apps later.

Most recent Galaxy Tab models take microSD cards up to 1 TB. Here is part of [Samsung's table of tablet memory card sizes](https://www.samsung.com/ie/support/mobile-devices/what-size-memory-card-does-my-galaxy-tablet-accept/).

| Model | Maximum external memory | Card type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Galaxy Tab S7+ | Up to 1 TB | microSD |
| Galaxy Tab S7 | Up to 1 TB | microSD |
| Galaxy Tab S7 FE | Up to 1 TB | microSD |
| Galaxy Tab S6 | Up to 1 TB | microSD |
| Galaxy Tab S5e | Up to 512 GB | microSD |
| Galaxy Tab S4 10.5 | Up to 400 GB | microSD |
| Galaxy Tab S3 | Up to 256 GB | microSD |
| Galaxy Tab A7 | Up to 1 TB | microSD |

The table covers UK and Ireland models. Samsung says specifications can differ by region, so check your model's spec page. Card capacity standards are covered in [SDHC vs SDXC](/blog/en/sdhc-vs-sdxc).

## How does a Galaxy Tab use the SD card?

As separate storage. [Samsung's microSD support page](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) covers both phones and tablets.

| Item | What Samsung says |
| --- | --- |
| Support | Not all Samsung devices support microSD cards |
| Adoptable storage | Samsung Android devices don't support it |
| File system | FAT32 or exFAT, with exFAT recommended |
| FAT32 | Only for cards under 32 GB, and no files over 4 GB |

Formatting, unmounting, and encryption are in the same Storage settings as on a Galaxy phone. The steps are in [Galaxy SD card slot and settings](/blog/en/galaxy-sd-card). Menu names vary by One UI version.

## Portable vs internal storage on other Android tablets

The difference is whether the card can move to another device. This comes from [Google's Android Help](https://support.google.com/android/answer/12153449?hl=en).

| | Portable storage | Internal storage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Save photos and videos | Yes | Yes |
| Install apps | No | Yes |
| Move the card between devices | Yes | No |

To set up a new card, back up first. Formatting permanently deletes the data on the card.

1. Swipe down from the top of the screen.
2. Tap the SD card detected notification.
3. For portable storage, select Format. For adoptable storage, select Format another way.
4. When formatting finishes, choose Move content or Move content later.

To switch a card you already use, open Settings > Storage > SD card and use the menu at the top right. Switching formats the card again. Menu names vary by manufacturer.

## What if my tablet doesn't detect the microSD card?

Work through these checks in order. They come from [Amazon's troubleshooting page](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GN5X9QEFQHZHZXLU) for Fire tablets, but the same order works on other tablets.

1. Check that the card's size is within your tablet's limit.
2. Unmount the card and insert it again.
3. Check that the card works in another device, such as a phone or another tablet.
4. Try a different microSD card.

If you plan to reformat, copy your files off first. Formatting on a computer is covered in [how to format a microSD card](/blog/en/format-microsd). For a card that still won't work, see [SD card not working](/blog/en/sd-card-not-working).

## Does the iPad have a microSD slot?

No. An iPad uses a card reader plugged into the charging port, and you open the card in Photos or Files. [Apple's camera adapter guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118280) lists microSD among supported cards. The steps are in [how to use an SD card with an iPad](/blog/en/ipad-sd-card).

To read a tablet card on an iPhone, check its format. [Apple's external storage guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios) lists APFS, Mac OS Extended, exFAT, FAT32, and FAT. The iPhone steps are in [how to use a microSD card with an iPhone](/blog/en/iphone-microsd).

## FAQ: Fire tablet memory card

### Can I use an exFAT card in a Fire tablet?

Amazon says Fire tablets only support the FAT32 file system. Its pages don't describe exFAT cards, so plan around FAT32. [Microsoft's file system comparison](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/fileio/filesystem-functionality-comparison) shows that FAT32 can't hold a single file over 4 GiB.

### Can I watch Prime Video downloads from the card?

Amazon says the Kindle and Prime Video apps only support portable storage. Set the card up as portable storage if you want to use it with those apps.

### Can I open an internal storage card on another device?

No. Both Google and Amazon say an internal storage card can't move between devices. To use it elsewhere, you have to reformat it, which erases the data.

### How big a card does the Galaxy Tab S7 take?

Samsung lists the Galaxy Tab S7 at up to 1 TB of microSD storage. Check your regional spec page, since Samsung says specifications can differ.

### Is the data on a tablet microSD card encrypted?

Microsoft's comparison table lists no file system level encryption for FAT32 or exFAT. Galaxy tablets have a separate SD card encryption setting. If you lose the card, someone else can read it, so store it carefully.
