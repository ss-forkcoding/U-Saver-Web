## Key takeaways

To move photos to an SD card on a Galaxy phone, open the My Files app, go to Internal storage > DCIM > Camera, touch and hold the photos, and move them. You can't do this from the Gallery app.

Moving deletes the original from the phone. For photos that matter, copy them instead, or keep another copy on a computer after you move them.

For photos you take from now on, change the storage location in the camera settings to the SD card. Some shooting modes still save to internal storage regardless of this setting.

"My phone is full, so I want to put my photos on the card." "I inserted a card, but my photos are still on the phone." These are common questions after adding an SD card to an Android phone. Inserting a card doesn't move existing photos on its own. This guide covers moving photos you've already taken and changing where new photos are saved. Inserting and formatting the card is covered in [micro SD card for phone](/blog/en/android-microsd).

## What should I check before moving photos to an SD card?

Check three things first.

| What to check | Why | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your phone has a microSD slot | Not all phones support microSD cards | [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) |
| The card is set up as portable storage | A card formatted as internal storage can't be moved between devices | [Google Android Help](https://support.google.com/android/answer/12153449?hl=en) |
| The card is exFAT or FAT32 | Samsung prefers exFAT and says FAT32 doesn't support files over 4GB | [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) |

The same Samsung guide says NTFS and APFS aren't compatible. If you plan to move long videos too, use an exFAT card. If the card needs reformatting, see [how to reformat a micro SD card](/blog/en/format-microsd). Formatting erases everything on the card.

## What's the difference between moving and copying?

Whether the original stays on the phone.

| Option | Original on the phone | Phone storage | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Deleted | Freed up | Clearing space on the phone |
| Copy | Kept | Unchanged | Keeping a second copy on the card |

[Samsung Korea's service guide](https://www.samsungsvc.co.kr/solution/39687) warns that if you tap cancel before a move finishes, or something unexpected happens, files being moved can be deleted. It also says service centers don't move data for you, so back it up yourself. For photos you care about, copy first, check that they open from the card, and then delete them from the phone.

## How do I move photos to an SD card with My Files on a Galaxy?

Camera photos are in the DCIM > Camera folder in internal storage. These steps follow [Samsung Korea's service guide](https://www.samsungsvc.co.kr/solution/39687) for Android 9 to 12.

1. Open the My Files app in the Samsung folder.
2. Tap Internal storage.
3. Open DCIM, then Camera.
4. Touch and hold the photos you want to move, then tap Move.
5. Go back up and tap SD card.
6. Choose a destination folder. The guide's example is DCIM > Camera on the SD card.
7. Tap Move here.

[Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) gives the same flow: touch and hold the files, tap Move or Copy, go back to the My Files home page, tap SD card, pick a folder, then tap Move here or Copy here. The SD card option only appears when a card is inserted.

To keep the photos in a new folder, [Samsung Japan support](https://www.samsung.com/jp/support/mobile-devices/how-do-i-move-data-between-the-external-sd-card-and-the-console-of-galaxy) shows how: open the SD card in My Files, tap More options (three dots) > Create folder, then move the photos there. Menu names can differ by model and One UI version.

## How do I move photos to an SD card with Files by Google?

Pick photos from a category, or use the Clean tab. These are the two methods in [Files by Google Help](https://support.google.com/files/answer/9712028?hl=en).

| Method | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| From Categories | Select a category → find the photo → More → Move to or Copy to → SD card → choose a folder → Move here or Copy here |
| From the Clean tab | Menu → Clean → Select files on the "Move to SD Card" card → choose files → Move to SD Card |

To move several photos at once, tap More > Select at the top right and check the circle next to each file. The same help page says Files by Google works on Android 8.0 and up.

## Why can't I move photos to an SD card from the Gallery app?

[Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001928/) says this move and copy method only works in the My Files app, not in other apps such as Gallery. If you find a photo in Gallery that you want to move, open My Files and find the same photo there. According to the folder notes in [Samsung Japan support](https://www.samsung.com/jp/support/mobile-devices/how-do-i-move-data-between-the-external-sd-card-and-the-console-of-galaxy), the DCIM folder in internal storage holds camera photos and videos as well as screenshots. Folder layout varies with the apps you use.

## How do I save new photos straight to the SD card?

Change the storage location in the Camera app. These are the steps from [Samsung UK support](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/using-an-sd-card/).

1. Open the Camera app.
2. Tap the settings cog.
3. Tap Storage location.
4. Tap SD card.

Some shots still go to internal storage with this setting on. [Samsung Japan support](https://www.samsung.com/jp/support/mobile-devices/how-do-i-set-the-location-of-the-galaxy-camera-to-an-external-sd-card) lists these exceptions.

| Shot type | Saved to |
| --- | --- |
| 8K video | Internal storage |
| UHD (60fps) video | Internal storage |
| GIF | Internal storage |
| Burst shots | Internal storage |
| RAW files | Internal storage |

The same page says you can still move these files to the SD card after shooting. Use the My Files steps above. Check the page's list of supported models.

## What if the SD card doesn't show up?

First check whether Settings recognizes the card. These steps follow [Files by Google Help](https://support.google.com/files/answer/9712028?hl=en).

1. Open Settings and tap Device maintenance (Device care) > Storage.
2. If the card isn't listed there, remove it and insert it again.
3. If Settings shows the card but the app doesn't, the card may be formatted as internal storage. Reformat it as portable storage.

[Samsung Korea's service guide](https://www.samsungsvc.co.kr/solution/39687) says that if the phone reports no card even though one is inserted, reinsert it or try it in another phone. If another phone can't read it either, the card needs checking. A fuller checklist is in [SD card corrupted or not recognized?](/blog/en/sd-card-not-working)

## How do I keep a second copy of the moved photos?

Connect the card to a computer and copy the photo folder. Cards can be lost or fail. If you moved the photos rather than copied them, the card holds the only copy. Unmount the card in Settings before you remove it. Connecting a card to a computer is covered in [SD card reader for PC](/blog/en/sd-card-to-pc).

## Can I move iPhone photos to an SD card too?

An iPhone has no card slot, so you connect a card reader. The steps and card formats are in [transferring photos from iPhone to SD card](/blog/en/iphone-photos-to-sd-card).

## Moving photos to an SD card: FAQ

### Can I move a whole Gallery album to the SD card?

Samsung US support says moving and copying only works in My Files. In My Files, open the folder that holds the album's photos, touch and hold to select them, and move them.

### If I set the camera to save to the SD card, do old photos move too?

The storage location setting in Samsung's guide applies to photos you take afterward. Move photos you've already taken with My Files or Files by Google.

### What happens if I cancel a move partway through?

Samsung Korea's service guide says files can be deleted if you cancel before the move finishes or something unexpected happens. Use Copy first for important photos.

### Why won't a video over 4GB move to the SD card?

Check whether the card is FAT32. Samsung says FAT32 doesn't support files over 4GB. Copy the card's contents elsewhere, then format it as exFAT.

### Can I move photos from the card back to the phone?

Yes. Open the SD card in the same file manager, select the photos, and move or copy them to internal storage. The steps mirror moving them to the card.
