## Key takeaways

When your iPhone storage is full, start by checking what's using the space in Settings > General > iPhone Storage. Then decide what to delete and what to move.

For apps, Offload App removes the app but keeps its documents and data. For photos and videos, make a copy on another storage device before you delete them.

Photos and videos you delete in the Photos app stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. To free the space right away, delete them there too.

"I keep getting an alert that my storage is almost full." "I can't take a photo because there's no space." "I deleted photos and the storage didn't change." These are common when iPhone storage is full. This guide covers what to do, from checking your storage to making copies before you delete, based on Apple's guides.

## iPhone storage full: what to do first?

These steps follow [Apple's guide to checking storage](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108429).

1. Open the Settings app.
2. Tap General.
3. Tap iPhone Storage. According to the [iPhone User Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/manage-storage-on-iphone-iph47c931112/ios), your available storage appears at the top of the screen.
4. Check the list of apps below to see which ones use the most space.
5. Tap an app's name to see more detail about its storage.

Apple notes that cached data and temporary data might not count as usage. If you see a "Storage Almost Full" alert, Apple suggests checking the storage recommendations or removing content such as videos and apps.

## What do the iPhone Storage categories mean?

[Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108429) splits storage into the categories below. The biggest category tells you where to start.

| Category | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| Apps | Installed apps and their content, the On My iPhone folder in the Files app, and Safari downloads |
| Photos | Photos and videos in the Photos app |
| Media | Music, videos, podcasts, ringtones, artwork, and voice memos |
| Mail | Emails and their attachments |
| Messages | Messages and their attachments |
| iCloud Drive | iCloud Drive files downloaded to your iPhone |
| Other | Siri voices, fonts, dictionaries, non-removable logs and caches, and system data |
| System | Space used by the operating system. It varies by device and model |

You can't remove most of what's in Other and System yourself. In most cases you free up space from content you created, such as apps, photos, and messages.

## How do you use the storage recommendations?

Recommendations may appear near the top of the iPhone Storage screen. According to [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108429), tap Show All, read each description, then tap Enable to turn one on. Tap a recommendation to review the content you can delete.

Apple says that when storage is low, your iPhone frees up space on its own by removing items you can download again or don't need, such as unused apps and temporary files. If that isn't enough, use the steps below.

## What's the difference between Offload App and Delete App?

On an app's detail screen, you can choose one of two options. This table follows [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108429).

| Option | What's removed | What stays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offload App | The storage the app uses | The app's documents and data |
| Delete App | The app and its related data | Nothing |

Offload App is the safer choice for apps you use now and then. To do it automatically, follow the [iPhone User Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/manage-storage-on-iphone-iph47c931112/ios): go to Settings > Apps > App Store and turn on Offload Unused Apps. Offloaded apps stay on the Home Screen with a cloud symbol, and tapping one reinstalls it.

## How do you reduce photos and videos?

If Photos is the largest category, move your photos and videos to another storage device first. These steps save originals to a USB drive, following [Apple's guide to downloading photos](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111762).

1. Connect the USB drive to your iPhone.
2. In the Photos app, go to your Library.
3. Tap Select, then choose the photos and videos to move.
4. Tap the Share button, then tap Export Unmodified Originals.
5. When the Files app opens to iCloud Drive, tap the Back arrow and choose the USB drive under Locations.
6. Tap Save.

When it finishes, open the USB drive in the Files app and open a few photos and one video. Delete them from your iPhone only after you've confirmed the copies open. Screen-by-screen steps are in [how to transfer photos from iPhone to USB](/blog/en/iphone-photos-to-usb), and the computer methods are in [the best way to save photos from iPhone without iCloud](/blog/en/iphone-photo-backup).

If the drive is FAT32, videos over 4 GB won't save. [Microsoft's file system comparison](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/fileio/filesystem-functionality-comparison) lists 4 GiB as the maximum file size for FAT32. If you have long videos, prepare the drive with [how to format a USB drive to exFAT](/blog/en/format-usb-exfat) and see [how to transfer video from iPhone to USB](/blog/en/iphone-videos-to-usb).

## You deleted photos but storage didn't change?

According to [Apple's guide to deleting photos](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/delete-or-hide-photos-and-videos-iphb4defbde9/ios), deleted photos and videos stay in the Recently Deleted collection for 30 days before they're removed permanently. To clear them sooner, follow these steps.

1. In the Photos app, tap Collections.
2. Tap Recently Deleted below Utilities, tap View Album, then unlock your iPhone.
3. Tap Select, then choose the photos and videos to delete.
4. Tap the menu button, then tap Delete.

You can't get these items back after this step. Check again that you have a copy on a USB drive or a computer before you delete them.

## What changes if iCloud Photos is turned on?

According to [Apple's iCloud Photos guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108782), when iCloud Photos is on, deleting a photo removes it from iCloud and from every device that uses iCloud Photos. If you delete photos just to free up space on your iPhone, they disappear from iCloud too.

If you plan to keep using iCloud Photos, you can turn on Optimize iPhone Storage instead. Your iPhone keeps space-saving versions, and iCloud keeps the originals. Go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Photos > iPhone Storage, then choose Optimize iPhone Storage. In iOS 26 or earlier, choose Optimize iPhone Storage directly on the Photos screen.

With this setting on, the originals may not be on your iPhone. Exporting them to a USB drive then takes time to download the originals, and Apple recommends connecting to Wi-Fi first. If your iCloud storage is also full and backups have stopped, see [iPhone not backing up?](/blog/en/iphone-backup-fails)

## What else can you delete besides photos?

The [iPhone User Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/manage-storage-on-iphone-iph47c931112/ios) lists these common sources of storage use.

| Item | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Messages | Delete conversations and attachments you no longer need |
| Downloaded videos | Remove videos you've already watched in the TV app and other streaming apps |
| Files and documents | Delete files you no longer need in the Files app |
| Apps | Delete apps you no longer use |

If a message attachment has a photo you want to keep, save it to the Photos app first. Deleted conversations are hard to get back.

## FAQ: iPhone storage full

### How do I see what's using the most storage on my iPhone?

Open Settings > General > iPhone Storage. Check your available storage at the top and the list of apps and their usage below.

### Does Offload App delete my data?

According to Apple, Offload App frees up the storage the app uses but keeps its documents and data. Delete App removes the app and its related data.

### I deleted photos, so why is my storage still full?

Deleted photos and videos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. After checking your copies, delete them there too.

### Can I delete Other or System storage?

According to Apple, Other includes non-removable logs and caches and system data. System is the space the operating system uses. Start with content you created, such as apps, photos, and messages.

### If I copy photos to USB, can I delete them from iCloud Photos?

You can decide once you've confirmed the USB copies open. But with iCloud Photos on, deleting a photo removes it from every device. The USB drive then holds your only copy, so store it somewhere safe.
