## Key takeaways

You can back up photos, videos, contacts, and files from an iPhone to USB. Each item type is saved separately from the Photos, Contacts, or Files app.

Apple's iPhone backup feature uses iCloud or a computer as its destination. Apple's guides don't describe a way to save a complete iPhone backup directly to USB.

There's no built-in way to move messages, app data, or iPhone settings to USB. To keep those three, also use iCloud Backup or a computer backup.

"Can I back up my whole iPhone to USB?" "Can contacts go on a flash drive too?" "What should I move before switching phones?" These are the first questions people ask when they look into backing up an iPhone to USB.

Backing up an iPhone to USB means copying the data you care about to a drive, one type at a time. This guide separates the steps for each type from the items USB can't hold.

## What can you save when you back up iPhone to USB?

When you back up iPhone to USB, you can save photos, videos, contacts, and files. Each type uses a different app.

| Item | Save to USB | App to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photos and videos | Yes | Export originals from Photos |
| Contacts | Yes | Export a contact card file from Contacts |
| Documents and downloaded files | Yes | Copy from Files |
| Messages | No built-in way | iCloud or computer backup |
| App data and iPhone settings | No built-in way | iCloud or computer backup |

Photos, contacts, and files fit on USB, while messages and settings don't. If you want to restore your iPhone exactly as it was after losing it, USB alone isn't enough.

## How to prepare a drive to back up iPhone to USB

exFAT is the safest format for an iPhone USB backup. FAT32 can't store files over 4GB, and NTFS isn't on [Apple's supported list](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios).

The drive needs more capacity than your iPhone's photos and videos take up. Open iPhone Storage under General in the Settings app to see how much space the Photos app uses.

If "Optimize iPhone Storage" is on for iCloud Photos, your originals are larger than the size shown on the iPhone. In that case, choose a drive based on the Photos size shown in your iCloud settings.

Connection options for each port are in [how to transfer photos from iPhone to USB](/blog/en/iphone-photos-to-usb).

## Steps to back up iPhone to USB by item

### Save photos and videos to USB

Select photos and videos in the Photos app and tap the Share button. Choose ["Export Unmodified Originals,"](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111762) then select your drive under Locations and save.

### Save contacts to USB

In the Contacts app, find the list you want and choose Export. Select the fields to include by following [Apple's guide to exporting contacts](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/export-contacts-iph075ddebf2/ios).

In the Share menu, choose Save to Files and pick your drive under Locations. Menu names can vary slightly by iOS version.

### Copy documents and files to USB

In the Files app, touch and hold the file you want to move and choose Copy. As in [Apple's guide to transferring files](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/transfer-files-iphone-a-storage-device-iphe9aff429a/ios), open a folder on the drive, touch and hold an empty area, and choose Paste.

## How is backing up iPhone to USB different from an Apple backup?

A USB backup copies files, while an Apple backup saves the state of the whole iPhone. The two restore different amounts of data.

According to [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/back-up-iphone-iph3ecf67d29/ios), iPhone backups are saved to iCloud or a computer. A computer backup includes almost all of the iPhone's data and settings.

Use an Apple backup to move everything to a new iPhone. Use a USB backup to keep your own separate copy of photos and contacts.

## Five checks after you back up iPhone to USB

If any of these five is a "no," don't delete the data from your iPhone.

- Photos on the drive open in the Files app.
- A long video plays through to the end.
- The contact card file is on the drive.
- The drive still has free space.
- The drive is stored somewhere safe in case it gets lost. Files copied to USB are stored as they are, and anyone can open them.

## FAQ: backing up iPhone to USB

### Can I back up my entire iPhone to USB?

Apple's guides don't describe a way to save an entire iPhone to USB. On USB, you save photos, videos, contacts, and files by item.

### Does an iPhone USB backup include messages?

No, messages aren't included. To keep messages, back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer.

### What size USB drive do I need to back up iPhone to USB?

You need a drive larger than the space your photos and videos take up. Check the Photos app's size under iPhone Storage in Settings first.

### Can I back up iPhone to USB without a computer?

Yes. iCloud and USB are compared in [how to back up an iPhone without a computer](/blog/en/backup-iphone-without-computer).

### Is the data in an iPhone USB backup safe?

Unless the drive itself is locked, anyone who has it can open the data. Keep a drive with your data somewhere that locks.
