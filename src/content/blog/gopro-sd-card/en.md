## Key takeaways

Choose a micro SD card for GoPro from the ratings in its manual. The GoPro MAX2 and LIT HERO manuals ask for a microSD card rated A2 and V30 or higher. The Insta360 X series asks for a UHS-I, V30 or higher card formatted as exFAT.

The two brands overlap but don't match. GoPro lists A2. Insta360 tells you not to use UHS-II or UHS-III cards. If one card will go in both cameras, check for all three marks: UHS-I, V30, and A2.

Cards wear out with use. Copy your footage off, format the card in the camera regularly, and swap in a new card if problems start.

"Can I put any micro SD card in my GoPro?" "Isn't a V90 card better because it's faster?" These questions come up when buying an action cam. This guide shows how to read the card requirements in official GoPro and Insta360 manuals and how to look after the card. It doesn't recommend specific products, and we didn't test any cards.

## What does a micro SD card for GoPro need?

Start with the SD card section of the GoPro manual. The [GoPro MAX2 manual](https://static.gopro.com/assets/blta2b8522e5372af40/blte32e2783ff44e36a/68c27e50d9e51a0e3481b803/MAX2_UM_en-US_REVA.pdf) lists these requirements.

| Item | GoPro MAX2 manual |
| --- | --- |
| Card type | microSD, microSDHC, microSDXC |
| Speed rating | Class A2 and V30 or higher |
| Maximum capacity | 1 TB |
| Maker | Use a brand-name card |
| Recommended cards | List at gopro.com/microsdcards |

The [GoPro LIT HERO manual](https://static.gopro.com/assets/blta2b8522e5372af40/blt5746f0817192c74e/68d4faef911e5d128655a6a3/LITHERO_UM_en-US_REVA.pdf) also asks for A2 and V30 or higher. Requirements can differ by model, so check your own camera's manual first.

A GoPro tells you when a card falls short. Both manuals say the camera alerts you if the card is rated below A2 and V30, or if it has degraded over time.

## What do A2 and V30 mean?

The marks describe different kinds of performance.

| Mark | Meaning | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V30 | Minimum write speed class for video | [How to Read SD Card Speed Classes](/blog/en/sd-card-speed-class) |
| A2 | App performance class for small reads and writes | [A2 SD Card vs A1](/blog/en/sd-card-a1-a2) |
| UHS-I | The card's bus interface | [UHS SD Card Guide](/blog/en/uhs-sd-card) |

"Up to XX MB/s" on the package is not a guaranteed minimum. Choose by the rating marks your manual lists.

## How is an Insta360 SD card different from a GoPro card?

The [Insta360 X Series memory card FAQ](https://onlinemanual.insta360.com/x6/en-us/specs/compatibility/sdcard) covers the ONE X through the X6.

| Item | Insta360 X Series FAQ |
| --- | --- |
| Speed rating | UHS-I, V30 or higher |
| File system | exFAT |
| Cards to avoid | UHS-II, UHS-III (recording may stop) |
| Maximum capacity | 1 TB for X6, X5, X4 Air, X4, X3, X2; 256 GB for ONE X |
| Slower cards | May cause recording interruptions or file corruption |

The same FAQ covers V60 and V90 cards. It says those ratings usually come on UHS-II or UHS-III cards, which may cause compatibility issues with X series cameras. A bigger speed number doesn't make a card a better fit.

The [Insta360 X5 manual](https://onlinemanual.insta360.com/x5/en-us/camera/basicuse/filestorage) recommends Insta360's own card and lists other cards tested as compatible. That list includes A1 cards, so Insta360 doesn't require A2.

## Can one card work in both a GoPro and an Insta360?

Yes, if it meets both manuals. Here's the comparison.

| Requirement | GoPro MAX2 and LIT HERO | Insta360 X series |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V30 or higher | Required | Required |
| A2 | Required | Not listed |
| UHS-I | Not listed | Required (no UHS-II or III) |
| exFAT | Not listed | Required |
| Maximum capacity | 1 TB (MAX2) | 1 TB (X2 to X6) |

For a card shared between both, look for UHS-I, V30, and A2 marks. When you move the card to the other camera, format it there. Formatting erases the card, so copy your footage first.

## How much capacity does an action cam card need?

It depends on resolution and bitrate. The [Insta360 X4 storage FAQ](https://onlinemanual.insta360.com/x4/en-us/faq/compatibility/storage) lists file size per minute.

| Resolution | Bitrate | Size per minute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8K 30fps | High | 1.5 GB |
| 8K 30fps | Standard | 1 GB |
| 5.7K 30fps | High | 700 MB |
| 5.7K 30fps | Standard | 500 MB |

The same FAQ says about 1 to 2 GB is reserved for recording cache. Dividing from the table, a 128 GB card holds roughly 80-some minutes of 8K high-bitrate video. That's simple arithmetic; actual time depends on the card's real capacity and your settings.

Stay under the maximum, too. The GoPro MAX2 and Insta360 X2 to X6 top out at 1 TB. Older models like the ONE X have lower limits.

## How do you choose an action cam SD card, step by step?

1. Find the SD card section in your camera's manual.
2. Note the speed class (V30), app performance class (A2), and bus (UHS-I) requirements.
3. Check the maximum capacity and pick a size that fits how long you record.
4. See whether the maker has a recommended card list. GoPro points to gopro.com/microsdcards, and Insta360 lists tested cards in its manual.
5. Buy from a trusted seller. The GoPro manual says to use a brand-name card. To check for cards with faked capacity, see [Fake SD Card: How to Spot One](/blog/en/fake-sd-card).
6. Put the new card in the camera and format it there.

For how this compares with regular camera cards, see [SD Card for Camera](/blog/en/camera-sd-card). For another device that records nonstop, compare [Micro SD Card for Dash Cam](/blog/en/dashcam-sd-card).

## How do you format a GoPro SD card?

Format it in the camera. The GoPro MAX2 manual gives these steps.

1. Swipe down on the screen to open the Dashboard.
2. Swipe left and tap Preferences > Reset > Format SD Card.
3. Tap Format to delete all files and reformat the card.

The GoPro manual says reformatting regularly helps keep the card in good condition. The Insta360 FAQ also recommends backing up your files and formatting the card regularly. Formatting erases everything, so move your footage first.

If you have to format on a computer, choose exFAT for an Insta360. For computer steps, see [How to Reformat a Micro SD Card](/blog/en/format-microsd).

## When should you replace a GoPro SD card?

The GoPro manual says SD cards can degrade over time and affect the camera's ability to save media. It suggests swapping an older card for a new one if you have problems.

Consider replacing the card when:

1. The camera warns about the card's rating or degraded performance.
2. The camera shows SD Card Error. The GoPro MAX2 manual says to restart the camera.
3. Recording still drops or files won't open after formatting.
4. Your computer doesn't detect the card. For troubleshooting, see [SD Card Corrupted or Not Recognized?](/blog/en/sd-card-not-working).

## How do you move action cam footage to an iPhone?

An iPhone has no card slot, so connect the card through an SD card reader. [Apple's external storage guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios) says iPhone reads formats including exFAT and FAT32. The exFAT format Insta360 asks for is on that list.

For connection steps, see [Micro SD for iPhone](/blog/en/iphone-microsd). To import into the Photos app from a reader, see [How to Use an iPhone SD Card Reader](/blog/en/iphone-sd-card-reader). Check the copied footage before you format the card in the camera.

## Micro SD card for GoPro FAQ

### Can I use an A1 card in a GoPro?

The GoPro MAX2 and LIT HERO manuals require A2 and V30 or higher. The camera alerts you if the card is rated lower. For other models, check that model's manual.

### Is a V90 card better than V30?

Not for the Insta360 X series. The Insta360 FAQ says V60 and V90 cards are usually UHS-II or UHS-III and may cause compatibility issues. It asks for UHS-I, V30 or higher.

### Can I use a card larger than 1 TB?

The GoPro MAX2 and Insta360 X2 to X6 manuals list 1 TB as the maximum. The Insta360 X4 FAQ says not to use cards larger than 1 TB.

### Can I move a micro SD card from my phone to an action cam?

Yes, if it meets the manual's requirements (V30, A2, UHS-I, and so on). Copy the card's files first, then format it in the camera. Formatting erases the phone's files.

### Can I use a high endurance dash cam card in an action cam?

Judge by whether the card's marks meet the action cam manual. "High endurance" alone doesn't tell you V30 or A2. Check the rating marks on the package.
