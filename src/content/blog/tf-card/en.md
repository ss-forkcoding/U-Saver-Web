## Key takeaways

A TF card is short for a TransFlash (T-Flash) card. SanDisk developed it, and the SD Association renamed it microSD when it adopted the format as a standard.

You can treat a TF card sold today as a microSD card. In 2005 the SD Association announced that TransFlash cards work in microSD devices and microSD cards work in TransFlash devices.

The TF name alone doesn't tell you the capacity standard or speed. Check the SDHC or SDXC, U3 or V30, and A1 or A2 markings on the card.

"The manual says to insert a TF card. Can I buy a microSD card instead?" This question comes up with action cameras, dash cams, and devices bought from overseas sellers. This guide explains what a TF card is and whether it is the same as microSD, based on SD Association documents. For how microSD compares with full-size SD, see [micro SD card and SD card](/blog/en/microsd-vs-sd).

## What is a TF card?

TF comes from TransFlash, also called T-Flash. The [SD Association's 20th anniversary article on microSD](https://www.sdcard.org/press/thoughtleadership/celebrating-20-years-of-microsd/) says SanDisk developed the card, and it was renamed microSD when the SD Association adopted and officially standardized it.

| When | What happened | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before 2005 | SanDisk shipped TransFlash cards for mobile phones | SD Association 2005 news release |
| July 13, 2005 | The SD Association announced final approval of the microSD specification | SD Association 2005 news release |
| After 2005 | Products that supported TransFlash moved to microSD | SD Association 2005 news release |

The [SD Association's July 13, 2005 news release](https://www.sdcard.org/cms/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/2005_07_13_sdcard.pdf) says more than 5 million TransFlash cards had shipped by then. The same release says existing TransFlash supporters would migrate their products to microSD.

## Is a TF card the same as a microSD card?

Yes, you can treat them as the same card. The 2005 release says the microSD card is fully compatible with SanDisk's TransFlash cards. It also says TransFlash cards operate in new microSD hosts and microSD cards work in existing TransFlash hosts.

| Item | TF card | microSD card |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Origin of the name | SanDisk's TransFlash (T-Flash) | SD Association standard name |
| How the name is used today | Common label on store pages and device manuals | Official name for cards and devices |
| Size | Fits the same slot as microSD | 11 x 15 x 1.0 mm |
| Interchangeable | Works in microSD devices | Works in TransFlash devices |

The size comes from the table in the [SD Association's speed class guide](https://www.sdcard.org/consumers/speed-class-standards-for-video-recording/). If a manual says "TF card slot," read it as a slot for a microSD card.

## What should you check when you buy a TF card?

TF and microSD are names for the card's size. Capacity and speed are marked separately. If a store page says only "TF card," look for these markings in the card photo.

| Marking | Meaning | Related guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| microSDHC, microSDXC | Capacity standard | [SDHC card vs SDXC vs SDUC](/blog/en/sdhc-vs-sdxc) |
| Class 10, U1, U3, V30 | Minimum write speed for video recording | [Class 10 SD card, U3, and V30](/blog/en/sd-card-speed-class) |
| UHS-I, UHS-II | Bus interface between card and device | [Types of SD cards](/blog/en/what-is-sd-card) |
| A1, A2 | Performance when apps run from the card | [A2 SD card vs A1](/blog/en/sd-card-a1-a2) |

The capacity standard must match what the device supports. According to the [SD Association's compatibility guide](https://www.sdcard.org/consumers/sd-sdhc-sdxc-and-sduc-card-capacity-choices/), SDHC devices use SD and SDHC cards, and SDXC devices use SD, SDHC, and SDXC cards. If an older device says it supports TF cards, check the maximum capacity in its manual first.

## How do you use a TF card in an SD slot or with an iPhone?

A TF card is the size of a microSD card, so it doesn't fit a full-size SD slot on its own. The [SD Association FAQ](https://www.sdcard.org/consumers/faq/) says microSD and SD cards differ only in form factor, size, number of pins, and the write-protect switch, and their logic and electronics are identical. That's why a microSD to SD adapter lets it fit an SD slot.

An iPhone has no card slot. You connect a card reader to the charging port.

1. Choose a reader that matches your iPhone's port. Use a USB-C reader for a USB-C iPhone and a Lightning reader for a Lightning iPhone.
2. If the reader has no microSD slot, put the TF card in an adapter and insert it into the SD slot.
3. Connect the reader to your iPhone and open the card in the Photos app or the Files app.
4. When copying is done, remove the reader.

The supported card list in [Apple's camera adapter guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118280) includes SDSC, SDHC, SDXC, miniSD, microSD, and MMC. [Apple's USB-C guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105099) also lists SD cards among devices you can connect. The detailed steps are in [micro SD for iPhone](/blog/en/iphone-microsd) and [how to use an iPhone SD card reader](/blog/en/iphone-sd-card-reader).

If a TF card from another device doesn't appear on iPhone, its format may not match. The steps are in [how to reformat a micro SD card](/blog/en/format-microsd). Formatting erases everything on the card.

## TF card FAQ

### What does TF card stand for?

TF is short for TransFlash, also written T-Flash. When the SD Association adopted the card as a standard, its official name became microSD.

### My device manual says TF card. Can I use a microSD card?

Yes. The SD Association announced that microSD cards work in TransFlash devices. Still, check the maximum capacity and recommended speed marking in the manual.

### Is a TF card slower than a microSD card?

The name doesn't decide speed. Compare the speed class, such as U3 or V30, and the bus interface, such as UHS-I, printed on the card.

### Can I put a TF card in an SD card slot?

Not on its own. A microSD to SD adapter lets it fit an SD slot. The adapter only changes the size, so check capacity compatibility separately.

### What if my TF card isn't recognized?

Check the adapter contact and the card format first. The steps are in [SD card corrupted or not recognized?](/blog/en/sd-card-not-working)
