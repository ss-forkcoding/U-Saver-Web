## Key takeaways

When iCloud storage is full, iCloud Backup, iCloud Photos uploads, and iCloud Drive syncing stop. Start by checking what uses the space in Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

You have two ways to get space back: delete what you don't need, or upgrade to an iCloud+ plan. Before you delete anything, copy the photos and files you want to keep somewhere else.

With iCloud Photos on, deleting a photo removes it from every device that uses the same Apple Account. If you're over your iCloud storage limit, deleted photos don't even stay in Recently Deleted.

"I keep getting an alert that my iCloud storage is almost full." "My iPhone says it can't back up." "I've used my free 5 GB. Do I have to pay for more?" These questions come up when iCloud storage runs out. If your iPhone itself is out of space, see [iPhone storage full? What to do first](/blog/en/iphone-storage-full). This guide covers what to do when iCloud storage is full.

## What stops working when iCloud storage is full?

According to [Apple's guide to managing iCloud storage](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922), you get 5 GB of storage for free when you set up iCloud. Backups, iCloud Photos, iCloud Drive, and more share that space. When it runs out, these features stop.

| Feature | When storage is full |
| --- | --- |
| iCloud Backup | Your device won't back up to iCloud |
| iCloud Photos | New photos and videos won't upload to iCloud |
| iCloud Drive and iCloud apps | They won't stay up to date across your devices |
| iCloud Mail | You can't send or receive email with your iCloud address |

Photos and files on your iPhone aren't deleted right away. But new content doesn't reach iCloud, so if you lose your iPhone, that content is gone. For fixes by error message when backups stop, see [iPhone not backing up?](/blog/en/iphone-backup-fails)

## How do you check your iCloud storage?

[Apple separates](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102670) iCloud storage from device storage. You check them in different places.

| Type | What it is | Where to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iCloud storage | Online storage tied to your Apple Account | Settings > [your name] > iCloud |
| Device storage | Storage inside your iPhone | Settings > General > iPhone Storage |

To check your iCloud storage, follow these steps.

1. Open the Settings app, then tap your name.
2. Tap iCloud. The amount of iCloud storage you've used appears near the top.
3. Tap Storage or Manage Storage to see usage by category, such as Photos, Backups, iCloud Drive, and Messages.

Start with the largest category. This table shows which section of this guide to read for each one.

| Largest category | Section to read |
| --- | --- |
| Backups | How do you reduce your iCloud Backup? |
| Photos | What to check before deleting photos in iCloud Photos |
| iCloud Drive, Messages, Mail | How do you clean up iCloud Drive, Messages, and Mail? |

## What's the fastest way to free up iCloud storage?

According to [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922), iOS 17 and later include Recommended for You in iCloud settings. It gathers things you might not need anymore, such as photos, large files, and old backups.

1. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap iCloud.
2. Tap Recommended for You.
3. Tap an option and follow the onscreen instructions.

If you don't see what you're looking for, delete content manually using the sections below.

## How do you reduce your iCloud Backup?

[Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922) suggests turning off backups for apps you don't use or deleting old backups.

**Turn off backups for apps**

1. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap iCloud.
2. Tap Storage or Manage Account Storage.
3. Tap Backups, then tap the name of the device you're using.
4. Turn off any apps you don't want to back up, then tap Turn Off to confirm.

Turning off an app removes all of its information from iCloud. Some apps are always backed up and can't be turned off.

**Delete old backups**

1. On the same Backups screen, tap the name of the device whose backup you want to delete.
2. Tap Turn Off and Delete from iCloud, then confirm.

Deleting a backup also turns off iCloud Backup for that device. Apple says iCloud backups stay available for 180 days after you disable or stop using iCloud Backup. You can't delete a backup that's currently being used to restore a device.

If you don't use iCloud Photos, your Photos Library is part of your iCloud Backup. Check its size under your device name on the Backups screen. If it's large, moving your photos to another storage device before backing up makes the biggest difference.

## What to check before deleting photos in iCloud Photos

[Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922) includes three points to know before you delete photos.

| Point | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Deletions sync everywhere | With iCloud Photos, a photo you delete on one device is deleted on every device signed in with the same Apple Account |
| Recently Deleted keeps items for 30 days | Items in this album don't count toward your iCloud storage |
| Over the limit, deletions are immediate | If you exceed your iCloud storage limit, photos and videos you delete skip Recently Deleted and can't be recovered |

So copy the photos you want to keep first. These steps save originals to a USB drive, following [Apple's guide to downloading photos](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111762).

1. Connect the USB drive to your iPhone.
2. In the Photos app, go to your Library.
3. Tap Select, then choose the photos and videos to copy.
4. Tap the Share button, then tap Export Unmodified Originals.
5. When the Files app opens to iCloud Drive, tap the Back arrow and choose the USB drive under Locations.
6. Tap Save.

If Optimize iPhone Storage is on, the originals may not be on your iPhone. Exporting then takes time to download them, so connect to Wi-Fi first. When it finishes, open the USB drive in the Files app and open a few photos and one video. Screen-by-screen steps are in [how to transfer photos from iPhone to USB](/blog/en/iphone-photos-to-usb), and the computer methods are in [the best way to save photos from iPhone without iCloud](/blog/en/iphone-photo-backup).

If the drive is FAT32, videos over 4 GB won't save. [Microsoft's file system comparison](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/fileio/filesystem-functionality-comparison) lists 4 GiB as the maximum file size for FAT32. If you have long videos, prepare the drive with [how to format a USB drive to exFAT](/blog/en/format-usb-exfat).

Once you've checked the copies, go to your Library in the Photos app, tap Select, choose the photos, and tap the Delete button. If you use an iCloud Shared Photo Library, photos in the Shared Library count toward the storage of the person who created it. To free your own space, make sure you're viewing your Personal Library.

## How do you clean up iCloud Drive, Messages, and Mail?

These steps follow [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922).

| Item | Steps | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iCloud Drive | Files app > Browse > iCloud Drive under Locations > More button > Select > choose files > Delete | Removes the files from iCloud Drive on all your devices. Someone else's shared folder doesn't count toward your storage |
| Message attachments | Tap the contact's name at the top of a conversation > Photos > Edit > Select Photos > choose images > Delete > Delete Attachment | Steps for iOS 26 or later. Applies when you use Messages in iCloud |
| Message conversations | Swipe left on a conversation, tap the Delete button, then tap Delete again | Deleted conversations are hard to get back |
| iCloud Mail | Mail app > iCloud inbox > More button > Cleanup Recommendations > iCloud Mail Cleanup | Keeps cleaning up that type of email automatically until you turn it off |

To keep copies of iCloud Drive files before you delete them, follow [Apple's guide to making copies of iCloud data](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108306). In the Files app, open iCloud Drive, tap the More button, tap Select, and choose the files. Tap More, then Copy Items. Open another location under Locations, touch and hold a folder, then tap Paste.

## How much does more iCloud storage cost?

If there's nothing you want to delete, upgrade to iCloud+. These are the US monthly prices from [Apple's iCloud+ plans and pricing page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108047), checked on November 16, 2026. Prices differ by country or region, and Apple notes that residents of some US states pay added tax.

| Plan | Monthly price (US) |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 5 GB, $0 |
| iCloud+ 50 GB | $0.99 |
| iCloud+ 200 GB | $2.99 |
| iCloud+ 2 TB | $9.99 |
| iCloud+ 6 TB | $29.99 |
| iCloud+ 12 TB | $59.99 |

After you upgrade, you're billed monthly. Every iCloud+ plan can be shared with up to five other family members. According to [Apple's guide to buying iCloud+](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108349), the steps depend on your iOS version.

| iOS version | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| iOS 27 and later | Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Storage (or Almost Full) > Get More Storage |
| iOS 18.4 to iOS 26 | Settings > [your name] > iCloud > scroll down > Upgrade to iCloud+ |

If you already have iCloud+, change plans in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Plan under iCloud+ Features in iOS 27 and later. In iOS 18.4 to iOS 26, go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > iCloud+ > See All Plans.

## FAQ: what to do when iCloud storage is full

### Does full iCloud storage delete photos on my iPhone?

According to Apple, a full iCloud stops backups, new photo uploads, iCloud Drive syncing, and iCloud Mail. Apple doesn't say photos on your iPhone are deleted. New photos just don't upload to iCloud.

### Is iPhone storage the same as iCloud storage?

No. iPhone storage is the space inside your iPhone. iCloud storage is online storage tied to your Apple Account. If your iPhone is out of space, see [iPhone storage full? What to do first](/blog/en/iphone-storage-full).

### Does Recently Deleted count toward my iCloud storage?

According to Apple, photos and videos in Recently Deleted don't count toward your iCloud storage. But if you're over your limit, deleted items skip this album and are removed right away.

### Can I delete the iCloud backup of an old iPhone?

You can if you no longer use that device and its content is stored somewhere else. Deleting the backup also turns off iCloud Backup for that device. You can't delete a backup that's being used to restore a device.

### Can I get by with the free 5 GB?

You can reduce usage by turning off backups for unused apps and by moving photos to a USB drive or a computer before deleting them. With many photos and videos, staying under 5 GB may be hard. Check your largest category first, then decide.
