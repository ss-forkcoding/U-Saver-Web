## Key takeaways

There are two ways to back up an iPhone without a computer: iCloud Backup and saving to USB. They differ in what they save and what you need.

iCloud Backup stores your iPhone's settings and data online. If you run out of iCloud storage, you have to buy more.

Saving to USB keeps copies of photos, videos, contacts, and files in your hands. There's no built-in way to move messages or iPhone settings to USB.

Choose iCloud Backup to restore your iPhone as it was, and USB to keep a separate copy of your photos. Using both means one still remains if the other goes wrong.

"I don't have a computer at home, so how do I back up my iPhone?" "I keep getting alerts that iCloud storage is full." "Can I store photos without paying every month?" These are common concerns for anyone trying to back up an iPhone without a computer.

You can back up an iPhone without a computer through iCloud or USB. A table below compares the two, followed by how to choose.

## What's the difference between the two ways to back up iPhone without a computer?

When you back up an iPhone without a computer, iCloud saves the state of the iPhone and USB saves copies of files. The costs work differently too.

| Comparison | iCloud Backup | Saving to USB |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What's saved | Information and settings on the device | Photos, videos, contacts, files |
| Messages and iPhone settings | Included in backup | No built-in way |
| What you need | Wi-Fi, iCloud storage | USB drive, an adapter if required |
| Cost | Buy more space if you run out | One-time purchase of a drive |
| Where the data lives | Apple's servers | Your own USB drive |

iCloud is better for restoring the whole iPhone, and USB is better for keeping a copy of your photos offline.

## How to back up iPhone without a computer using iCloud

In the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then iCloud Backup. Turn on "Back Up This iPhone" and tap Back Up Now to start right away.

According to [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/back-up-iphone-iph3ecf67d29/ios), iCloud backs up automatically every day when the iPhone is connected to power and Wi-Fi and is locked. Plugging in the charger before bed means there's less to think about.

[iCloud Backup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108366) doesn't include items already synced to iCloud. If iCloud Photos is on, your photos are stored in iCloud Photos separately from the backup.

When you see a low-storage alert, you need to buy more space by following the on-screen steps or reduce what's backed up. If photos and videos take up most of your space, look at moving copies to USB as well.

## How to transfer photos from iPhone to USB without a computer

When backing up with USB, you save each type from the Photos, Contacts, or Files app. For photos, choose "Export Unmodified Originals" and select your drive under Locations.

The detailed steps are split up in [how to transfer photos from iPhone to USB](/blog/en/iphone-photos-to-usb) and [how to back up iPhone to USB](/blog/en/backup-iphone-to-usb).

exFAT is the recommended format. FAT32 can't store files over 4GB, and NTFS isn't on [Apple's supported list](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios).

## Which way should you back up iPhone without a computer?

To back up an iPhone without a computer, first decide how much you need to be able to restore. If two or more of these four apply, it's worth adding USB.

- You often see alerts that iCloud storage is low.
- Photos and videos take up more than half of your iPhone's storage.
- You want a copy you can view without internet access.
- You don't want to raise your monthly costs.

If you want messages and settings back after losing your iPhone, keep iCloud Backup on. USB doesn't replace iCloud Backup. It gives you one more copy of your photos and contacts.

## FAQ: backing up iPhone without a computer

### Can I back up my entire iPhone without a computer?

iCloud Backup saves your device's information and settings without a computer. USB holds only copies of individual items.

### Do I need Wi-Fi to back up iPhone without a computer?

Automatic iCloud backups need Wi-Fi. Saving to USB moves files already on your iPhone with no internet connection, but photos stored only in iCloud have to be downloaded first.

### Can I back up iPhone without a computer for free?

If you already have a USB drive, you can move photos with the iPhone's built-in apps at no extra cost. With iCloud, you have to buy more space if you run out.

### Does backing up iPhone to USB without a computer save messages?

No, messages aren't saved. To back up messages, use iCloud Backup or a computer backup.

### Can I restore later after backing up iPhone without a computer?

An iCloud backup is restored when you first set up an iPhone. Photos saved on USB can be saved back to Photos from the Files app.
