## Key takeaways

Learning how to use an SD card starts with your device. Check that it supports the card's capacity standard (SDHC, SDXC, or SDUC) first.

Format a new card in the device that will use it. Cameras format from the camera menu, Android phones as "portable storage," and computers with the SD Association's formatter.

Before you remove the card, eject it or use Safely Remove Hardware. Pulling it out mid-write can cost you data.

"Can I just put a new SD card in and start using it?" "Should I format it, or is that a bad idea?" People ask these questions the first time they use a card. This guide walks through what to do with a new SD card, in order. If you want to know about card types first, read [types of SD cards](/blog/en/what-is-sd-card).

## How to use an SD card: what do you do first?

Break it into five steps.

| Step | What to do | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Match the card to your device | A device can't use a card standard it doesn't support |
| 2 | Check the lock tab and the direction | A locked card won't accept new files |
| 3 | Format it in the device that will use it | This prepares the card in a format the device expects |
| 4 | Save a file and copy it elsewhere | This confirms the card reads and writes correctly |
| 5 | Eject before removing | Removing it mid-write can lose data |

## Can your device use this SD card?

Look for the SD logo on the device and check the manual. [The SD Association's capacity guide](https://www.sdcard.org/consumers/sd-sdhc-sdxc-and-sduc-card-capacity-choices/) says to confirm compatibility through the device's SD logo or user manual before inserting a card.

| Card | Devices that can use it |
| --- | --- |
| SD | SD, SDHC, SDXC, and SDUC devices |
| SDHC | SDHC, SDXC, and SDUC devices |
| SDXC | SDXC and SDUC devices |
| SDUC | SDUC devices only |

An older device that supports only SDHC can't use an SDXC card. The capacity ranges are in [SDHC card vs SDXC vs SDUC](/blog/en/sdhc-vs-sdxc). If you doubt that a new card really holds what the label says, see [how to spot a fake SD card](/blog/en/fake-sd-card).

A microSD card needs an adapter to fit a full-size SD slot. See [micro SD card and SD card differences](/blog/en/microsd-vs-sd).

## How do you insert an SD card?

Check the lock tab, then insert the card the right way.

1. Make sure the lock tab on the side is in the unlocked position. When locked, the card blocks saving and deleting. Details are in [SD card lock switch](/blog/en/sd-card-lock-switch).
2. Insert the card the way the device manual shows. [Apple's guide to the Mac SD card slot](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102352) says the metal contacts should face down and point toward the computer.
3. Don't force it. Apple warns that forcing media into the slot might damage it.

If your computer or phone has no SD slot, use a card reader. See [how to choose an SD card reader](/blog/en/choose-sd-card-reader).

## Should you format a new SD card before first use?

Formatting it once in the device that will use it is recommended. Formatting erases everything on the card. If the card is used or came from another device, copy any files you need first.

| Where you'll use it | Official guidance | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera | Format a card in the camera the first time you use it | [Sony camera Help Guide](https://helpguide.sony.net/ilc/2210/v1/en/contents/TP1000843993.html) |
| Android | Format it as portable storage or internal storage | [Android Help](https://support.google.com/android/answer/12153449?hl=en) |
| Computer | Use SD Memory Card Formatter rather than OS tools | [SD Association formatter](https://www.sdcard.org/downloads/formatter/) |

Sony's Help Guide says to format the card on the camera. It warns that a card formatted on a computer may not be usable, depending on the format type. For other camera brands, check the format menu in your manual.

The SD Association says operating system formatting tools may not be optimized for SD cards and may lower performance. Computer steps are in [how to format an SD card](/blog/en/format-sd-card).

## How to use an SD card on an Android phone

A notification appears when you insert the card. These steps follow [Android Help](https://support.google.com/android/answer/12153449?hl=en).

1. Swipe down from the top of the screen.
2. Tap the SD card detected notification.
3. Select Format for portable storage, or Format another way for internal storage.

| Mode | Photos and videos | Install apps | Move to another device |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Portable storage | Yes | No | Yes |
| Internal storage | Yes | Yes | No |

Menu names may differ by manufacturer. [Files by Google Help](https://support.google.com/files/answer/10857645?hl=en) recommends formatting the card as portable storage. If you plan to move the card to a computer or camera, choose portable storage.

## How to use an SD card on a computer or iPhone

A computer connects through an SD slot or a USB reader. [The SD Association FAQ](https://www.sdcard.org/consumers/faq/) says to use a USB reader if the computer has no SD slot or its slot follows an older standard than the card.

An iPhone has no card slot, so it needs an SD card reader. [Apple's guide to external storage](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/external-storage-devices-iph95baac91f/ios) says the device must have a single data partition and use exFAT, FAT32, FAT, APFS, or macOS Extended. You can import photos from the card straight into the Photos app.

## How do you remove an SD card safely?

Eject first, then remove it.

1. On a Mac, drag the card's icon to the Trash. [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102352) warns that removing a card while the Mac is asleep could lead to data loss.
2. On Windows, right-click the Safely Remove Hardware and Eject Media icon in the taskbar and eject the card. [Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/safely-remove-hardware-in-windows-1ee6677d-4e6c-4359-efca-fd44b9cec369) says to always safely remove devices before disconnecting them to avoid losing data.
3. On a camera, don't remove the card while the access lamp is lit. Sony's Help Guide gives this warning for formatting.

## What if a new SD card isn't recognized?

Check these in order.

1. Remove the card and insert it again. Apple says a card may not mount if you insert it too slowly or too quickly.
2. Confirm you inserted it as the manual shows and that the device supports the card's standard.
3. Note any error message and look it up in the manual. These are the basic steps in [the SD Association FAQ](https://www.sdcard.org/consumers/faq/).
4. If it still fails, contact the card maker or the seller. More checks are in [SD card corrupted or not recognized?](/blog/en/sd-card-not-working)

## FAQ: how to use an SD card

### Can I use a new SD card without formatting it?

You can, but formatting it once in the device that will use it is recommended. Sony says formatting a new card in the camera gives stable performance.

### Can I format a camera card on my computer?

Format it in the camera. Sony's Help Guide says a card formatted on a computer may not be usable in the camera, depending on the format type.

### Can I move an Android internal-storage card to another device?

Android Help says a card formatted as internal storage can't be moved between devices. To move it, reformat it as portable storage, which erases its data.

### Can one card hold photos, videos, and documents together?

Yes. The SD Association FAQ says you can save data in any format as long as the card has space. Playing it back needs a device that supports that format.

### How long does an SD card last?

The SD Association FAQ says normal use typically gives a card a lifespan of 10 years or more. It depends on the product and how you use it, so keep important photos on a second storage device too.
